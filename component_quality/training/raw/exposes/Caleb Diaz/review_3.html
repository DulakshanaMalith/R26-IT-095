The exposé shows effort in trying to motivate the topic, but the introduction and motivation are not clearly structured. The different parts of the story do not connect well. For example, the switch from COVID related learning disruptions to holograms in the year 2000 feels sudden and unrelated. A clearer storyline and connection between ideas would make the motivation stronger and is needed.

Throughout the paper, there are several unsupported claims. These need to be backed up with proper references. Some of the conclusions also seem questionable or not logically derived from the arguments. For example, using capital growth in the AI market as proof of potential for AI backed holographical learning in education is not convincing.

The research questions are too broad and unfocused. They read more like an overview of a whole research area than concrete questions for one study. The last question is not really a research question at all. It would fit better in a blog post than a research paper.

The state-of-the-art section needs more focus and citations to connect to the research topic. It is not clear how theories like TPACK relate to the work.

The methodology does not match the research questions and is too complex. It includes too many disconnected components, making it unlikely that the studies could answer the research questions.

Minor comments:

Some parts sound AI-written, while others contain spelling errors. The paper also needs polishing to improve flow and consistency. For instance, “this study” is used in confusing ways to introduce unrelated ideas.
