The exposé sets up the idea that holographic educators powered by AI (artificial intelligence) could replace human educators in the future and even improve the teaching in dimensions which humans are not capable of, like accessiblity in difficult situations.

It tries do indentify key areas for improvement by doing surveys and doing experimental studies.

The SOTA (state of the art) shows that there have some advancements on the topic, but that theres space to do specialized resarch in.

Some sub-rqs (research questions) are too broad and unspecialized to have a conclusive answer. For example "what are the opportunities and difficulties?" has large amounts of dimensions where it can be applied. The difficulties part could for example refer to legal and ethical difficulties but also to technical difficulties. Opportunities could be financial or scientific or functional for large amounts of humans etc. Ideally try to specialize the rqs so that the appropriate answer is fully covering the question and no topic is left out.

In the theoretical frameworks part only theoretical non-technical frameworks are listed. One could also specify the technical aspect as to shine light on possible technical opportunities and difficulties. Like for example architectural aspects.

The methodology is based on human judgement, with little objective surveilance. The surveyed participants could be biased, like educators not wanting to be replaced. Developing a weighing framework, for giving different weights to different answers, would be ideal, in order to bring some functional modeling into the subjectiveness. Furthermore assessing long term results by comparing datasets of people who studied with this technology and those who didnt could be an interesting follow up.

The schedule is very tight with little buffer room. There are also months with greater than 4 weeks and ones with less. Having a set number of weeks like for example 3 per month, with the rest of the time being a buffer could provide dynamic feedback through self-assessment in comparing the descriptions of the weeks/months and the current work progress.

In future research try to ideally be more structured in the methodology, so that results can be, to some degree, reproducable. This would help when cooperating with other researchers as they could continue to work on these topics and reproduce your results themselves.

Furthermore try being more dynamic with your schedule, too strict schedules could hinder your ability to branch into new fields when confronted with unexpected results.

Your motivation on the other hand was written well as it supported the idea by showing its relevance in todays world and prior successes with similar approaches. Keeping it a little shorter might be helpful for space management, but other than that its fitting as is.
