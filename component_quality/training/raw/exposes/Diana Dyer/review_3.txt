Summary:
US citizens do not visit doctors as much as it would be needed due to factors like a low perceived need to go to the doctor, costs etc. A more accessible alternative would be the use of LLMs which motivates this study, investigating the accuracy and understandability of treatment plans of an LLM finetuned towards muscle injuries in football players in order to understand how well LLMs can be used as an alternative to doctors in specific cases.
Strengths:
You have a very good motivation to the topic, showing a clear research gap
Your research questions are well scoped, well defined and it is clear how you plan on answering them
Your methodology seems suitable and you justify your methods well
You show a high level of detailedness and know your topic very well 
Weaknesses:
Not only should existing/previous work be mentioned, but also shown how your methods/research differentiates from them. In your 'Approach' section, your transition to your research is not as clear as it could be. Focus on showing the contrast between existing work and your work.
A theoretical framework that you base your thesis on is important to explain in an exposee. Unfortunately, you did not develop such a framework. Even though it may seem difficult to fit in a framework into your thesis, it is a vital part of an exposee and therefore should be included after the SOTA part.
Other:
Great work all in all!
