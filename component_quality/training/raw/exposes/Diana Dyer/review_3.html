Summary:

  • US citizens do not visit doctors as much as it would be needed due to factors like a low perceived need to go to the doctor, costs etc. A more accessible alternative would be the use of LLMs which motivates this study, investigating the accuracy and understandability of treatment plans of an LLM finetuned towards muscle injuries in football players in order to understand how well LLMs can be used as an alternative to doctors in specific cases.
