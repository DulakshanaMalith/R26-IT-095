Summary

The exposé proposes a bachelor-level research project that investigates how Large Language Models (LLMs) can be integrated into a single “teaching agent” capable of recommending personalized learning paths for students. Motivated by the rapid rise of tools such as ChatGPT and Copilot, the project argues that tailoring instruction to individual cognitive profiles could transform learning from a struggle into an engaging experience.

Strengths

  • Timely & relevant topic – personalised LLM tutoring is cutting-edge and clearly valuable in education.
  • Clear end-goal – you specify the desired outcome (an agent that assembles learning paths) and outline an iterative “collect → refine → repeat” cycle.
  • Basic academic formatting – LaTeX template used and page limit respected.
  • Initial theoretical anchor – adopting Colace et al.’s metadata formula shows awareness of foundational adaptive-learning work.
  • Preliminary timeline exists – even if it needs polishing, tasks are mapped week-by-week.

Weakenss

  • Hook & domain specificity – opening lacks a vivid example and does not state which subject area or learner group you’ll study.
  • Problem context thin – current approaches and their shortcomings are not described in detail.
  • Research question is too broad – needs narrowing to something answerable within a bachelor timeframe.
  • State-of-the-art review missing – no systematic survey or gap analysis of recent LLM-tutoring literature.
  • Novelty unclear – your unique contribution beyond integrating existing tools is not yet articulated.
  • Methodology under-specified – data sources, metrics, ethical steps, and risk mitigation need concrete detail.
  • No discussion of practical challenges/limitations – potential pitfalls (e.g., small sample size, model bias) and backup plans are absent.
  • Schedule realism – key tasks overlap and block descriptions are brief; buffer time for ethics and recruitment is missing.
  • Bibliography placeholders only – real references and consistent citation style still required.

Language & flow – some long sentences and shifting terminology weaken clarity; tighter editing recommended.
