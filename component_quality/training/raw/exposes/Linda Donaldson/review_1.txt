Summary:
This exposé examines how generative AI, in particular LLMs, can be effectively integrated into existing healthcare systems to generate personalised treatment plans. The aim is to address the technological and ethical challenges by conducting a systematic literature review and empirical phase.

Strength:
A well-organised exposé is crucial for clarity and readability. Your text is convincingly organised and arouses the reader's interest with its clear structure and interesting introduction. This makes it easier to follow the theme and to understand the aim of the work.
In scientific papers, it is important to provide up-to-date examples and explanations of the reasoning. You have done this very well. 
The methodology describes how the individual research questions are answered. The methodological approach is clearly structured. The general implementation of the methods is well explained and detailed. 

Weakness:
The motivation should explain to the reader why the chosen topic is relevant and which weakness the thesis wants to address. The problem is addressed in your exposé, but there is no in-depth explanation of its relevance. It remains unclear which specific problems are to be solved by personalised treatment plans and what consequences the existing challenges such as data fragmentation or algorithmic distortions have for patients and the healthcare system. Read your text again carefully and try to explain the current deficits and their consequences more clearly. Also think about which current gap your research should close and try to incorporate this into your motivation. 
A well-defined research question is important for a thesis in order to ensure that the research has a precise focus. There is a research question in your text, but it is formulated as a statement. In order to create more clarity and sharpen the focus, the questions should be presented as concrete questions. In addition, add sub-questions to your main research question to structure your work better. 
In the approach, it is important to summarise the state of the art. Your text does not sufficiently explain the advantages of personalised treatment plans. Try to emphasise more clearly which improvements these plans bring for patients and medical processes. It also remains unclear why the current weaknesses are particularly problematic. The negative consequences of these shortcomings for patients and the healthcare system should be described more clearly in order to emphasise the urgency of the issue.
Within the theoretical framework, the theories used in the work are presented and their relevance justified. The technical details used in the work are not sufficiently described. It remains unclear which specific AI models or theories are used and how the main challenges such as bias or data integration are to be addressed technically. It would be helpful to mention existing frameworks or approaches on which the work is based or to explain how these will be further developed. 
The methodology describes how the individual research questions are answered.  The target group of the qualitative interviews is not sufficiently defined in the description of the methodology. It remains unclear who exactly is meant by ‘healthcare professionals’ - whether they are doctors, nursing staff, radiologists or other specialist groups.  Think about a clearer target group for your research. 
The time schedule is used to plan the thesis in advance to see whether it is possible in the short time available. There are gaps in the schedule, particularly with regard to the writing phase. While the literature review and the empirical phase are well covered, there is no clear description of how the writing of the individual chapters is planned. Think about what parts a thesis consists of and when the writing of these best fit into the schedule.
