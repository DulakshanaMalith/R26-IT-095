The proposal uses an unusual way to motivate the work by asking the reader to "Imagine this scenario". This is not a strong way to start, as it allows the reader to imagine a situation where the problem does not exist. It would be better to directly explain that the problem is real and important.

The research question is not clearly defined. The phrase "How can LLMs be enhanced" already suggests that LLMs are currently able to adapt to learners’ emotional and cognitive states, but this is not introduced or explained earlier in the proposal. This needs better motivation and clearer explanation.

In general, the motivation includes many claims that are not supported. For example, saying things like "most", "a lot of them", or "whether due to emotional challenges, motivation issues, or different cognitive styles—are at risk of falling further behind." These statements need sources to back them up. This problem of unsupported claims continues throughout the proposal and strongly affects the quality of the work.

The citation style in the text is inconsistent. Sometimes it uses AUTHOR et al. (YEAR), and other times it uses [INDEX]. Please choose one citation style and use it consistently. Also, when using author names in the text, still include a bracket citation.

There are also some unclear phrases. For example, the proposal refers to "this research" and "the study", but it's not always clear if this means the student’s own work or work by others (sometimes uncited).

In the "State of the Art" section, existing frameworks are mentioned but not named. It's important to name them and clearly show how the proposed work is different or new. In the "Validation and Testing" section, there is a mention of "traditional adaptive systems", but again, no sources are given.

Also, the description of how the system will be analyzed is too general. "Statistical tools" is a vague term. It would be better to say what kind of tools or tests are planned (e.g. T-test, Bayesian analysis, etc.).

The "Practical Considerations" chapter feels disconnected from the rest of the proposal. It focuses heavily on privacy concerns, which does not seem to be the main goal of the project.

The timeline also has some problems. The first month includes tasks like "search and critically analyze research on adaptive learning systems" and "define specific, clear RQs", which should already be done before writing the proposal. Also, terms like "Draft concepts" and "prepare datasets" are unclear. It would help to say what these should look like by the end of the scheduled time.

Finally, there are several typos throughout the proposal, which should be fixed for better readability.

Overall, the work needs better grounding in related work and some clarification.
