Summary

This thesis investigates the application of AR glasses powered by large language models (LLMs), addressing key questions about technical constraints, potential use cases, and user experience.

Strengths

  • Relevance: The exposé focuses on a compelling and timely real-world use case for AI technology, showcasing its potential applications in AR devices.
  • Accessibility: The use of clear, relatable examples makes the proposed research approachable and helps emphasize its significance and practical relevance.

Weaknesses

  • Writing Style:
    • A scientific exposé should employ formal, concise, and precise language. This draft falls short in several areas:
      • Informality: Phrases such as "I wonder how..." and "Even tho" are inappropriate for academic writing.
      • Errors: There are consistent typos (e.g., "ChatGBT" instead of "ChatGPT," "neeed"), placeholders (<LINK>, <Example?>), and stylistic inconsistencies.
    • A revision to eliminate informal language, correct errors, and refine overall clarity would significantly enhance the quality.
  • Scope:
    • The research questions lack precision and fail to align with the scope of a typical ~6-month thesis project.
    • Refining the scope to focus on 1-3 clear and actionable research questions would improve feasibility and make the exposé more structured and impactful.
  • Approach:
    • The Approach section is underdeveloped and lacks essential components:
      • State-of-the-Art (SOTA): There is no discussion of existing research, leaving the exposé disconnected from the current knowledge base.
      • Research Gap: The thesis does not clearly identify the gap it aims to address, which weakens its justification.
      • Framework & Experiments: The methodological plan is vague and lacks specifics about theoretical foundations or experimental design.
      • Citations: The absence of references undermines the credibility of the work.
    • This section requires a complete overhaul, incorporating a thorough literature review, a clearly defined research gap, and a detailed methodology with proper citations.
  • Schedule and Bibliography:
    • The Schedule remains a placeholder and does not outline the timeline or milestones for the thesis.
    • The absence of a Bibliography further detracts from the academic rigor of the document.
    • Completing these sections with realistic timelines and a comprehensive list of references is essential for a professional and complete exposé.
