Summary:

The proposal aims to enhance AI assistants by integrating holographic visualization and expanded input methods, such as gesture and object recognition, to improve interactivity and usability for everyday use.

Strength

1) The exposé follows a logical structure

2) The exposé effectively highlights the gap in current AI assistant technology

Weaknesses

  • (Content)

The proposal mentions the general limitations of current AI assistants and their current features to underline the importance of visualizations. However, it lacks of specific examples of SOTA AI assistants. Provide examples of current AI assistants and how their limitations in visual input or 3D visualisation impact user interaction.

  • (Language)

The language in scientific work should be precise, formal, and objective to communicate ideas clearly and credibly, without trying the reader to convince. However, in this proposal there are some areas where the language could be further refined. For example avoid using “we”, “our”. Instead, consider make it more neutral, objective. I recommend reviewing the text to eliminate subjective language.

  • (Language)

In general, using long sentences could make the ideas harder to understand for readers. It is highly recommended to use short and understandable sentences. For example in the second paragraph of the motivation the sentences could be separated into more clear and short ones. On the other hand, there are a lot of short paragraphs, which could be added to each other. Try to use shorter sentences and edit the paragraphs.

  • (Hook)

The introduction and teaser sections could benefit from a stronger, more engaging hook to immediately capture the reader’s attention and clearly highlight the significance of the proposed work.

The introduction jumps into facts about AI assistant usage and limitations without offering a compelling, attention-grabbing statement or scenario that immediately makes the reader excited about the potential of this research.

Start with a hook that immediately presents the significance of the research in a relatable way.
