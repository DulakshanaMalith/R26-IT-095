The exposé  begins with an interesting question, "What if your colleague were a hologram instead of a person?" This creative introduction successfully captures the reader's attention. Nevertheless, the shift from this engaging opening to specific real world problem could be made smoother. Consider adding a brief sentence connecting the scenario to the wider technological issues or by adding a statistical data by referencing the current study or research which targets a real life problem and acts as a hook to draw user's attention.
The problem statement or teaser identifies a clear gap in current systems, specifically highlighting the limitations associated with flat-screens interactions and the insufficient application of holographic technology. The claim "They don't fully leverage the immersive potential of holography" is strong, however supporting the claim with relevant examples or data would significantly strengthen its validity. For example, referencing the existing limitations in a current used system or technology could make the gap more concrete.
-The significance of holographic AI assistants is articulated very well in the beginning,  particularly regarding their capacity to facilitate interactive and human-like experiences.

The research questions are nicely and closely aligned with the identified issue and shows a balance between practical and theoretical concerns. They give raise to critical thoughts and cover essential aspects such as efficiency, useability, ethical concerns etc. In my opinion the ethical question, "What are the potential ethical concerns regarding data privacy and security in holographic AI systems?" can be restructured to point to practical solutions, such as  "How can data privacy and security risks in holographic AI systems be effectively addressed?"
Overall, the questions show a strong relation with the study's goals and are situated within the context of the current literature.

The use of "Embodied Cognition Theory" as the theoretical framework is a remarkable strength of this expose. It provides a solid basis for studying holographic AI, highlighting the critical role of physical and environmental interactions in cognition. The connection between this theory the proposed system is nicely put together, especially the argument that 3D interactivity can significantly improve both learning outcomes and task execution.
In order to strengthen this section more:-
Adding a small example of how Embodied Cognition Theory has been applied in similar technological contexts to provide a support for the argument.
Clarify some terms such as " vermicular AI systems" so that reader can easily understand the context.

The methodology is thoroughly articulated and aligns well with the research objectives. The integration of qualitative approaches, such as interviews and case studies alongside quantitative techniques like surveys ensures comprehensive data collection and analysis.
