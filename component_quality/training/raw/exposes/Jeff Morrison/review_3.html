Summary

This expose tries to investigate whether a radiologist with the help of AI systems can predict interval breast cancer using DBT scans better than two radiologists without AI. It points out that existing researches only compare the performance of AI systems with digital mammography and not DBT scans. To tackle this, the author plans to get the dataset from a cancer center, conduct a quantitative reader study with the double radiologists without AI as the control group, and perform statistical analysis on the result of the control versus study group.

Strength:

  • The expose has good and concise definitions when medical-related keywords are brought up.
  • The amount and quality of citations are overally good, with citations backing up most statements in the expose
  • SOTA section gives reader sufficient context about the current landscape of research

Weakness:

  • In the motivation section, there are multiple factual inconsistencies. The motication section should serve as an introduction for the readers to have an overview of what's currently achieved, what's missing and what would you handle in this project. However, you have multiple contradictory and inconsistent statements, which couconfuse readers and don't make the problem overview clear. For example, you claim "AI for DM outperforms radiologists", "DBT offers higher accuracy", but "AI for DBT performs worse than radiologists". This is confusing, because what would "higher accuracy" for DBT mean, if it makes AI systems perform worse?. I suggest rephrasing this part and cross-checking the cited sources to ensure factual correctness and avoid misunderstanding.
  • A sub research question should aim to make it clearer and easier to answer the main research question, but I personally feel you miss important sub-RQs that are relevant for the main RQ that you're researching. For example, neither in the sub-RQ nor in the methodology did you mention effect of potential biases in the data and how you plan to address it, even though data biases could lead to big performance differences in AI systems. I suggest reconsidering the research questions and address the missing parts explicitly, to ensure the project is well-rounded.
  • A theoretical framework should be given so that the reader understands in what grounds is your research built on. However, this section is completely missing from your expose. I suggest adding this, and tying it with the research questions and methodology.
  • Which RQs and sub-RQs are handled in which part during the methodology and schedule section should be noted down explicitly, to ensure all RQs will be answered in time and using correct methodology. However, it is missing from your expose. I suggest adding this back in.

Others:

Normally, writing multiple drafts of the thesis as the research is going on could help with understanding the current state of the research and identify problems or any potential things missed during the planning phase. You pushed the writing phase at the end, after all research is done, which also means you have to write and intepret your research as it is, without having the ability to run further experiments to clear up any result confusion.
