The motivation misses the point of teasing what kind of facial expressions are going to be explored. The human face has a huge number of facial expressions. Testing all of them will be too much for a single scientific work. The author should narrow it down a bit and at least tease what type of facial expressions the user can expect. Also, this should be reflected in the RQ.

Besides that, the author could have a look into the advantages and disadvantages of within- and between-subject designs, as I can’t find a good explanation of why a between-subject design is necessary here. The author states the limitation of finding enough participants to fulfil the selection criteria. Using a within-design could help out.
