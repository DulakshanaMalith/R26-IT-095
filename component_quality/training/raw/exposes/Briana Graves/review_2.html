Summary:

This research aims to explore the ethical and legal challenges of deploying AI for skin cancer diagnosis without compromising patient safety and trust.

Strengths

The proposal is very detailed.

Weaknesses

  • (Theoretical Framework)

The theoretical framework, while present, lacks sufficient depth. Expand on specific ethical theories or frameworks. Include spesific theories and how these theories guide the proposed frameworks.

  • (Schedule)

The schedule is too general and lacks specific timelines(weeks), and connections to the research questions and sub-questions. The schedule should explicitly align with the research questions and sub-questions. To improve the schedule, break it down into weeks, add the research questions and give the buffers between the research weeks and at the end for general delays.

  • (Chapters and Sections)

The proposal is structured into one chapter and multiple sections. However, the division does not make much sense. To make it easier for the reader, try to change the structures similar to recommended. (1-Motivation: it includes the hook, anchor, teaser,  research questions, 2-Approach: it has the SOTA, theoretical framework and methodology. The bold ones are for section names)
