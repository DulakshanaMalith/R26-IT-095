Summary

In your proposal "Holographic AI Assistants in Museums: Simulating Historical Personalities," you notice that museums nowadays need to develop modern ways to attract audiences using modern interaction methods. To solve this task, you propose developing an AI assistant trained in historical papers and articles to simulate historical personalities and visualize them with a hologram. Users could interact freely with them to learn more about history. In your BA, you plan to examine these holographic AI assistants' needed technical background and additional societal aspects.

Aspect 1

In your motivation, you wrote that since the COVID-19 pandemic, museums faced a dramatic decline in visitor numbers. You aim to motivate your topic by providing an up-to-date example and an alarming development. This is important to make the reader clear why you think that this topic is relevant.

To ensure you provide valid and important facts, you should always provide a citation or source of such claims. This ensures that the reader will trust your judgment and understand the importance of your work. In this case, you do not provide any source or citation for the claim. For a sophisticated paper, you should avoid having unproofed claims in your work. I would suggest citing some paper that proves your claim with statistics. This will lead to a better, more understandable paper and avoid having false claims in your work, which could be an undesired situation.

Aspect 2

In the paragraph about already existing work and state-of-the-art and similar research projects, you provide a short overview of aspects that have already been examined in this field of work. The goal is to give the reader an overview of aspects that have already been examined and why your research question is new and has not already been answered by another paper. This should include the most relevant recent papers to give the reader a better understanding of the cutting-edge research.

As the state of the art, you provided two papers about holographic and 3D visualization in museums, one from 2003 and one from 2020. They seem to be a relevant part of the research in this field. But especially the first paper from 2003, which uses mostly different techniques for holographic visualization than used today, may not be cutting-edge research, as the paper was published 20 years ago. To extend your knowledge and get a more detailed overview of this research area, I would suggest extending your literature review to ensure that you do not miss other more recent papers about your topic. This also ensures that your research question is a new contribution in this field.

Aspect 3

You mention that these holograms might be able to replicate human emotion and behavior if the AI uses a context-based personality. Your goal is to justify the use of the social presence theory in your project. However, it is not clear why and how the AI might be able to replicate human emotions. You do not mention any ambitions to give the AI the skills to simulate emotions. For a reader, it is not obvious why the AI might be able to do that. To allow the reader of your paper to follow your thoughts, you should avoid saying that some aspects might be possible without providing more details about what kind of aspects will decide if something will or won't work.

Aspect 4

I appreciate that you try to follow a scientific writing style. A scientific writing style is important for the reader to follow your thoughts and avoid unscientific opinions. While the writing style is always a personal preference, I would avoid the extensive use of passive speech in your papers. Passive speech makes the text harder to understand and the sentence structure more complex. Often, it is better practice to use the scientific "we" instead. To improve your writing style, you may want to look at the book "The Elements of Style" by Strunk, W., Jr. and White, E.B., or other style guides. This allows you to self-reflect on your current style and further improve it.

Aspect 5

To gather knowledge, you plan to do expert interviews. This is important to get domain-specific insights and extend your knowledge. To ensure that you get bias-free data, the group of experts should be as diverse as possible. The idea of interviewing experts with experience with digital

museum exhibits and six PhD students from the AI or vision and human-computer interaction is a good starting point. However, it is not clear how many interviews you plan to conduct and how exactly you want to choose the interview partners. You write that "these are equally distributed" but not in which aspect they are distributed. You also plan only to use a relatively small number of participants, which is acceptable for this scope of a BA. However, you also do not provide any details on how you plan to select your participants in the testing phase. To show the reader that you follow scientific principles by choosing representative interview partners, you may want to add some details about how you plan to choose the partners and which criteria you plan to apply in the decision process. On the other hand, your plan to use semi-structured combined with open-ended and closed questions appears to be a good method for interviews.
