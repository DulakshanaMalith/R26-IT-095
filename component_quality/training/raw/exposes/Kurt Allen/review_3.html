Incorporation of Key Elements

The exposé includes various critical components, such as motivation, State of the Art (SOTA), research questions, methodology, and ethical considerations. However, some vital elements are either inadequately elaborated or missing. For example, there is a lack of an attention-grabbing hook accompanied by relevant statistical data.

Clarification of Issue Importance

The document underscores the importance of the issue by highlighting the communication gap between healthcare providers and patients during the perioperative phase. It points out that poor communication may lead to misunderstandings and adverse patient outcomes. While the significance is apparent, the argument could be strengthened by including specific examples or data to demonstrate the extent and effects of this issue. For instance, statistics regarding patient dissatisfaction or complications post-surgery linked to communication failures would make the problem more tangible.

Shortcomings in Current Literature

The exposé notes a gap in the current academic literature, indicating that while there has been extensive research on AI in diagnostics and drug development, its role in perioperative communication has not yet been explored. However, this point is only briefly addressed, and the connection between existing studies and the proposed research is not fully articulated. The SOTA section should delve deeper into past methodologies and results from other studies, clearly identifying their shortcomings and explaining how this research intends to overcome them.

Alignment of Research Questions with the Problem Statement

The research questions are aligned with the problem statement, focusing on applying generative AI to improve communication. Nevertheless, the questions lack precision. Phrases such as “reducing misunderstandings” and “significance” are vague and need to be defined with measurable criteria. This ambiguity could hinder the ability to assess the project's outcomes. To improve alignment, the questions should be reformulated to reflect clear, actionable objectives.

Elimination of Uncertainty in Terminology

Although the research questions are relevant, they contain ambiguous terminology that needs clarification. For example, “minimizing misunderstandings” could be more explicitly phrased as “restricting misunderstandings.” Similarly, the term "meaningful questions" should be defined using specific parameters, such as their relevance to patient care or the clarity of their formulation. Clarifying these ambiguities would enhance the project's lucidity and evaluability.

Introduction of Ideas and Principles

The exposé does not adequately introduce theories or concepts related to communication between patients and providers

or decision-making. Incorporating established models, such as shared decision-making frameworks or theories of patient engagement, would provide a stronger conceptual foundation for the study. Doing so would enhance the research's credibility by grounding it in recognized theoretical structures.

Methodology

The chosen methodology, which consists of interviews and scenario-based testing, is considered appropriate for addressing the research questions. However, the explanation lacks specifics. For example, the exposé does not detail how the scenarios will be designed, how the interviews will be conducted, or how the data will be analyzed. Including approaches like thematic analysis for qualitative data or evaluation frameworks for AI-generated inquiries would improve the methodology. Furthermore, it is unclear how the effectiveness of the open-source AI models will be assessed, including the criteria for evaluating the quality of the questions generated.
