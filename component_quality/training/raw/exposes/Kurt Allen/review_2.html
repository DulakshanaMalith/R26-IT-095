The Motivation needs a bit of an overhaul. For instance, the first sentence states that “Perioperative information consultation […] is one of the crucial points before and after surgery” without citing a source. While this might be obvious for experts in the medical field, for a computer scientist, this is a statement without justification. Next, “these information exchange needs to be done in a balanced two-way conversation […] to limit misunderstandings” also needs clarification. Is it the only way? This is suggested by the text, but it could also be the best way for non-experts. The wording should be tuned down a bit to show the importance, while not overclaiming to be the only valid solution out there. Also, the SOTA chapter starts off with a bit of overclaiming and “clickbait”.

The Motivation also leads towards the research gap that the paper wants to address. However, the exposé fails to really assert why the proposed way is probably a good solution and how these gaps need to be urgently addressed. The connection to the first part of the motivation is missing.

The Research Questions some work. RQ1 and sub-RQ1.1 are relatively closely connected. At the same time, sub-RQ1.2 is a yes/ no question that needs to be avoided as a research question.

The theoretical framework needs some clarification on what the LLM is actually going to be used for. Is it just used to answer questions, so the doctor does an initial consultation and the LLM answers some basic questions, or function as a notebook, as one sentence suggests? Also, will the patient be “pushed” to the LLM as soon as possible? How will patients react to not being able or allowed to talk to a human anymore?

The methodology also has similar issues. Why are university students picked for interviews? Are only they considered as patients? Also, the paragraph states we “try to answer our research questions”. You will answer them. You don’t try to. Otherwise, you missed the point of your whole work. Also, this paragraph fails to talk about the issue of LLMs hallucinating answers. This is a serious issue in healthcare. Why are only general “artificial intelligent agents” considered? Why no fine-tuned or subject matter models?

Lastly, the last quarter of the methodology does not make sense to me. The English is hard to read, and I don’t understand the setup of the study at all. What is actually achieved and why? Why are there 10 students, and then another 10 to ask questions? What are “some criteria”? What evaluation are you going to do? “Ethical and privacy issues will not be handled on this paper as they are at the beginning of their journey with these artificial intelligent systems [9, 10]”, so we just ignore ethics for now? Why not the Wizard of Oz method?
