This research investigates how integrating holographic AI assistants into augmented reality systems can enhance user interaction through a sense of physical presence. The goal is to provide an understanding of digital assistants' current capabilities and limitations and analyze how humans engage with interactive systems. Furthermore, it will explore the possibility of using holographic AI to improve trust, engagement, and efficiency in practical applications such as education and healthcare.

The proposal presents an innovative and relevant topic. Several key strengths particularly stand out:

To begin with, the goal of a hook is to capture the reader's attention and set the stage for the topic. The introductory quote "AI agents will become our digital assistants, helping us navigate the complexities of the modern world. They will make our lives easier and more efficient." creates engagement and interest from the very beginning of reading the proposal. This quote effectively captures the relevance of AI assistance in today’s society and the expectations for future development. It's a fantastic entry point for this research. To further strengthen this, referring back to it in the conclusion might tie it better into the work.

Also, the research questions should be clear, focused, and well-structured to guide the study. Sub-questions should explore different aspects of the main question. These research questions provide a well-defined direction for this study, focusing on improving user interaction by integrating holographic AI into augmented reality. The sub-questions dive deeply into the topic, particularly the technological requirements, user perception compared to traditional digital assistance, and improved task performance and efficiency in applications. These questions help guide the proposal and ensure consistency.

Another point is that theoretical frameworks should provide a foundation for the research, contextualizing it within existing knowledge and help with the interpretation of results. The selection of the theoretical framework represents another key strength. The Media Equation Theory aligns greatly with the focus on user interaction and provides a well-established lens for analysis of human interaction and perception of AI systems. This framework grounds the proposal in proven theories and enhances its credibility.

Lastly, the methodology should be practical, detailed, and directly aligned with the research questions to ensure the study can achieve its objectives effectively. The proposed methodology corresponds well with the objective of this research, combining literature reviews, user studies, and usability evaluations to conduct a thorough investigation. This approach is feasible and practical. The mixed-methods approach is very balanced and addresses both the technical and the user-centric aspects of the research. It is well-suited for exploring this topic.

The proposal demonstrates a clear understanding of the topic and a well-structured approach. However, there are a few areas where the proposal could be revised to ensure greater clarity and effectiveness:

First, the title and subtitle should communicate the focus and scope of the research, drawing attention while providing direction. While the current title is very captivating, the subtitle lacks a concrete direction to fully complement the proposal. A fitting subtitle enhances the overall paper by summarizing the focus and guiding the reader on what to expect. "The Holographic AI Assistant" may be the main theme, but it doesn't indicate the concrete focus of the paper, such as the integration of holographic AI assistants into augmented reality. The current combination of title and subtitle does not allude to any of the aspects addressed in the paper. A revision of the subtitle to include the key aspects of the paper would provide a clearer idea of the content and strengthen the total package.

Second, defining technical terms is essential to ensure clarity and accessibility, especially for interdisciplinary research where terms may be interpreted differently by various audiences. The proposal could be further enhanced by providing definitions for the key technical terms. Terms like "augmented reality" and "holographic AI" are central to this research but lack a definition which could result in misunderstandings about their specific use in this study. Including brief definitions for key terms in the introduction part of the paper or as footnotes would enhance the clarity for a broader audience.

Furthermore, the State of the Art (SOTA) section plays a vital role in demonstrating the novelty of the research by situating it within the context of existing work. The current explanation of the SOTA is underdeveloped, lacking detailed examples or an in-depth exploration of current advancements in holography, AR, and AI. This weakens the justification for the study's significance. Additionally, relying on a reference from 1997 is insufficient, as it does not reflect the relevant technological advancements in these fields made in current years. Expand the SOTA section to provide a deeper review of current technologies and research, incorporating examples and references from the past five years. Further elaborating on the limitations of the existing digital assistant examples can emphasize the importance of your study. 

Finally, the usage of credible references is critical for establishing the validity of the research and enhancing the paper's academic quality. The citation of Wikipedia as a source on the Likert scale undermines the proposal's credibility, as it is not considered a reliable reference. While it may be useful for a first understanding, it should not be cited in an academic paper. Replacing the citation with a reference from peer-reviewed articles or textbooks on the Likert scale ensures that the proposal is grounded in reliable sources, thereby strengthening its academic credibility.

To conclude, the text is coherent and easy to follow, yet addressing the outlined areas would further enhance its clarity and academic quality.
