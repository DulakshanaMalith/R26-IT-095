Summary
The proposal investigates the impact of gamification (points/levels) on engagement and learning outcomes in LLM-PATs. It employs a mixed-methods approach (quantitative study + qualitative interviews) to address three sub-RQs, focusing on engagement dimensions, learning outcomes, and user characteristics. The theoretical framework on gamification for learning and controlled experimental design are central to the methodology.

Strengths
With a clear learning goal, this proposal aims to understand how gamification influences engagement and learning outcomes in the context of LLM-PATs for Higher Education. Several concrete sub-research questions have been proposed for exploration.
The literature review for existing studies is extensive.
The methodology part is clear and well-defined grounded on a theoretical framework, which provides a robust foundation for the proposal.

Weakness
The concept of the engagement learning paths, gamification, and LLM-based tutoring systems lacks fluidity. The connection between them has not been illustrated thoroughly. The introduction of the approach can be moved to the motivation to enhance the fluidity.
To remove people with experience with LLMs or gaming frequency might not be an ideal choice. There is no guarantee they can learn better than other students. It also depends on how the game is designed in this project.
The methodology design needs further elaboration. For instance, it lacks some details about what courses or learning tasks will be used for evaluation. The design of LLM-based Personalized Adaptive Tutors is not described clearly. To improve it, you can give specific learning tasks and the details of the designed LLM-based tutors.
