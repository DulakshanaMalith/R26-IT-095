Summary:

Many professional fields become increasingly more complex. This study aims at investigating the potential increase in operational efficiency, decision-making and innovation when incorporating holographic AI assistants

Strengths:

  • You address the limitations of your methodology well
  • You describe a good set of data sources for your review

Weaknesses:

  • Citing properly is extremely important for scientific work. You have many sentences that would require a citation, since you claim them to be true, and they could be considered plagiarism without citations. It might help to go over your text and think for every sentence whether it is an unsupported claim (with need for references) or your own work/assessment of something
  • The choice of research question(s), and a limited scope sets the overall tone and structure of your expose and, later, your thesis. You have a very wide scope for your research questions, and your sub-RQs broaden the scope even further. Think of it the other way around - the sub research questions should be more specific and help narrowing down and answering the main research question.
  • Related to the point before - Your methodology should clearly show how you will answer your RQs. As you chose your RQs to be really wide, your methodology will not provide answers to all of them, let alone a subset of them. Keep the overall goal of your research in your mind when designing the methodology part so that you make sure you can answer all (sub-)parts of your research questions. This will also ensure that you have a good structure.
  • The more concise and correct your writing style, the better. You tend to use words or expressions that sound nice, but are not relevant or scientific in this context (e.g. "groundbreaking", "transformative" about your own work), or especially filler sentences without a lot of meaning (e.g. "could enhance the fit by addressing challenges" -> what challenges, "In doing so, it positions itself at the intersection of AI, AR, and holography, offering transformative solutions that address current solutions" etc). When going through your text, think about what expressions and sentences you just used as filler sentences, and which actually provide additional value.
