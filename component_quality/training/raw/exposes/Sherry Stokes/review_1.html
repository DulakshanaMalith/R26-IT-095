Summary:

The exposé examines the use of artifical intelligence in education, particularly how AI tools can support teachers and improve the learning experience for students to address the current challenges. The aim of this study is to analyze the current state of AI integration in school, assess its impact on teaching and the learning atmosphere, and investigating how these technologies can be further developed in the future to improve education.
