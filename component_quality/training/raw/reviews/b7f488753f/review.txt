The ethical considerations are mentioned broadly but could be expanded to include specific principles like beneficence, non-maleficence, and justice in the context of AI in healthcare.
Suggestion: Tie these ethical principles explicitly to the challenges discussed, such as algorithmic bias or reduced human oversight.

The expected outcomes are ambitious but could be more specific about how the proposed framework will address identified barriers.
Suggestion: Provide examples or scenarios illustrating how the framework could be applied in real-world clinical settings.
