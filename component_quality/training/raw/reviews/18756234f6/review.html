Summary:

The proposal explores how holographic AI assistants integrated into augmented reality (AR) can enhance user interaction by addressing current limitations in digital assistant design, with applications in education and healthcare.

Strengths

The proposal follows a logical flow

The hook seeks attention.

Weaknesses

  • (Credibility)

In scientific writing, one of the most important things is citing the claims and ideas to provide evidence, demonstrate engagement, give credit to the work of other researchers and make your own work more credible for readers. The proposal lacks of citations to support the claims made. For instance, The claims about AR’s potential and the shortcomings of current AI systems are not supported by data, studies, or benchmarks. Without citations, the points you raise come across as opinions rather than evidence-based insights, which can weaken the academic value of your work. Consider citing your claims in your motivation and SOTA.

  • (Theoretical Framework)

The framework introduces the Media Equation Theory as its foundation but fails to provide sufficient depth in explaining its core principles. It requires a deeper explanation of its foundational aspects. For instance, this concept is not fully unpacked or contextualized for the reader. Provide an explanation how it describes human responses to technology and include examples or applications of the theory in similar contexts.

  • (Methodology)

The methodology is well-aligned with the proposal, but it would benefit from more specificity in describing the literature review process, sampling strategy, data analysis etc. For example, it is not mentioned how the participants will be selected and which difficulties could occur. By improving these gaps, the methodology will gain greater clarity and academic credibility.
