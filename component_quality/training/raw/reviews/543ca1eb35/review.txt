The introduction starts off with a great motivation and leads towards an interesting topic. However, it has some minor things to address. First, citing the DFKI book should be done in the same way as other papers were cited. Second, the introduction states that teachers “just give the correct answer” instead of explaining why the students make these kinds of errors. However, the paper does not credit a source here. Similarly, later on, the text talks about several applications without naming a single one of them. Finally, the research questions are quite messy. The first one gets presented in line with the text, while subsequent ones get listed. Make this uniform and iterate over them. Especially the first one is not well formulated with dropping in random side nodes in brackets. This is bad style and leads to research questions that are not suitable to be answered.

The state-of-the-art chapter needs a bit of polishing and refinement. Some cited sources do not really correlate with the proposed topic, e.g. [7]. Also, the author again puts important information in brackets rather than talking about it in the full text. Further, while the theoretical framework presents a number of relevant and important models, the text fails to fully lead from one model to another and does not present a clear connection between them and how they relate to the author's own proposal. Here, the text needs more focus and not just a listing of things out there. For example, the connection to the primary school came as a surprise and as a “side-note” while being a very important, unique selling point.

The methodology is very vague. While some points, like recruitment from a Darmstadt school, … are very specific, it does not handle challenges very well. For instance, the exposé states: “Maybe it will be necessary to split the class into smaller groups”. This is a huge deal and makes the difference between a between-subjects and within-subjects design. Both require special handling and can not easily be swapped out. Also, the relation to the research questions is not that clear. Is the prompting important or just something that needs to work?

Minor things:
-The schedule is hard to understand since it uses really short descriptions and is missing details. I could not fully grasp the schedule.
-The paper mixed multiple citation styles and has some typos. These should be fixed in the next version.
