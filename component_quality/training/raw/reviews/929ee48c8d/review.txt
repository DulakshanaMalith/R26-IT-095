Summary:
The aim of this thesis is to investigate how Generative AI can be used in healthcare to develop medical diagnoses and treatment plans, and to support related decision-making processes. For this purpose, medical data of patients will be collected in compliance with privacy regulations and used by an AI model to develop diagnoses and treatment plans. The results of the AI model will be compared with real diagnoses and treatment plans made by human doctors to understand how and whether generative AI can be beneficially applied in this use case.

Strength 1:
A strength of the methodology is its emphasis on the privacy-compliant collection of medical data. Although there are laws and regulations that dictate how sensitive personal information, such as medical data, can be used (as stated in the text), it is important to mention compliance with these privacy practices in the exposé to increase confidence in the work.

Weakness 1:
As the first part of the exposé, the purpose of the hook is to make a particularly good impression on the reader, using appropriate wording to surprise, shock, interest or otherwise grab their attention. Facts and figures used in the hook should be taken from reliable sources and referenced to support the credibility of the exposé.
However, the first sentence of the hook 'Medical errors cause over 250,000 deaths' is not well written as it is not clear what country or time period the deaths referred to occurred in. There is also no reference to explain the source of this information.
You should rephrase the hook and add more context to your first statement, such as the location and time period of the deaths, so that the reader understands the text and doesn't lose interest. You should also include a reference directly in this sentence to support your point.

Weakness 2:
After the hook, the anchor should lead into the explanation of the problem at hand, explaining why the problem is relevant and should be addressed through research. To do this, it is important to describe the domain of the problem and explain how the problem is currently being addressed.
But the anchor is not sufficiently detailed in the text, and it does not explain why research should be carried out into the use of generative AI in healthcare to develop diagnoses and treatment plans.
You should expand the anchor and describe in more detail what the problem is that your research aims to address and solve. You could try to answer the following questions with your anchor: How are diagnoses and treatment plans currently developed? What specific features does Generative AI have to support this process? Is Generative AI already being used in this process, or is it not yet established?

Weakness 3:
The research question should be precise and narrowly focused. It should state concisely and clearly what exactly needs to be investigated, without being too broad. It should also be answerable using scientific methodology.
However, the motivation section contains a very broad and general research question that is not narrowly related to a research objective. 
You should try to formulate the research question more precisely to make it clearer what answers your work will provide. For example, the term 'assist' is very vague, and you could narrow it down by specifying whether you want to investigate improving the efficiency or accuracy of diagnosis with Generative AI. You could also focus your work and research question on either diagnoses or treatment plans, making the question more specific. 

Weakness 4:
The purpose of the SOTA section of the approach is to place your own work in the context of the research environment in the subject area. It does this by describing how related work has addressed the problem and how your own work and methodology differs from related work. Weaknesses of related work are identified to show why further research is needed in this area. In addition, improvements and strengths of your own approach are highlighted to emphasize the relevance of your own work.
The SOTA section of the text is too vague and not detailed enough. It lacks a precise explanation of the procedures and methods used in other studies and a precise description of the weaknesses of these procedures. Gaps 'in privacy concerns and the integration of Generative AI into clinical workflows' were identified, but this brief mention is too unspecific.
You should expand your SOTA section by explaining in more detail how related work has proceeded to address the problem, i.e. what methodology has been used. To illustrate the relevance of your own work, you should mention weaknesses in that methodology that you do not repeat in your work. You can also mention differences and unique strengths of your approach, e.g. that you plan to pay particular attention to privacy and use a novel form of data anonymization (if one exists).

Weakness 5:
A detailed and transparent description of the methodology supports the validity of the results found in a research study. It is important that the methodology is fully described so that it is clear how the answer to the research question was found. 
The methodology in the exposé is not sufficiently detailed and contains gaps, so that it is not clear how the research question will be answered. 
To complete the methodology, you should describe how you will obtain the patients' medical data and how it will be passed to the AI model, e.g. in a specifically worded prompt. You should also explain what quantitative data you mean by the 'quantitative part of the method', e.g. the agreement rate of diagnoses with medical experts. You should also explain what the role of the medical experts is when they compare their own diagnoses with those of the AI model.
