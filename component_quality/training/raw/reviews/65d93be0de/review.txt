1. Summary
The exposé deals with the development of personalized treatment plans through the use of generative AI. The aim is to find new treatment methods that increase success through specific strategies. A focus is placed on the analysis of data to create diagnoses and medication suggestions in real time. It is discussed whether the integration of generative AI in medicine is possible and whether life-threatening decisions can be entrusted to it. The aim of the exposé is to raise awareness of the potential benefits and challenges of generative AI in healthcare.

2. Strength
The exposé describes the challenges of current, generalized treatment methods well and highlights the urgency of developing patient-specific solutions. In particular, the emphasis on increasing efficiency in emergency situations and the support provided by AI makes the work highly relevant. However, it also emphasizes that research still needs to be done, as it is not so easy to let AI decide over people's lives.
The exposé addresses important topics such as the acceptance of AI in the healthcare sector. These not only reflect current challenges, but are also relevant for the future development and widespread use of AI in medicine.
The motivation is well presented by describing concrete problems, such as the success of treatment through individual patient data, and also by addressing how generative AI and its potential can help. The overview of existing progress in the integration of AI in healthcare strengthens the argument.

3. Weaknesses
Unclear target definition of the project: The aim of an exposé is to formulate a clear and feasible research question and the overarching goal of the project. The relevance of Generative AI is well emphasize, but the concrete definition of the goal remains vague. The main question ("How can Generative AI increase the success rate of medical treatments through patient-specific treatment strategies and what does the rise of Generative AI in medical care depend on?") is promising, but there is a lack of clear delimitation, e.g. whether the focus is on emergency situations, medication, the detection of diseases such as cancer or the integration of AI into everyday clinical practice. It would be useful to narrow this down even more precisely in order to focus specifically on these areas. Formulate the objective more clearly by naming the specific area of application (e.g. detection of diseases such as cancer) and specifying the expected results. A clear delimitation helps to focus the work on the essentials and thus make it easier to understand
Lack of methodological concretization: An exposé should not only highlight the problem and objectives, but also explain which methods are to be used to answer the research question. This helps to check the feasibility of the project and helps you to implement it. The exposé mentions general advances in AI and outlines approaches such as the use of imaging techniques. However, it lacks a clear description of the methodology, how you want to analyze data yourself and which methods you want to use to work it out. It is not clear how you intend to answer your research questions. Add a detailed description of the methodological approach, e.g. which data sources are to be used and how you intend to deal with them. What are the metrics and success rates you set yourself to answer the research questions. It is also best to go into each individual research question and mention which methodological approaches you want to use for this. This would better illustrate the feasibility of the project.
Data protection is not addressed: Your third research question is about patient data protection. However, you don't really address this in the exposé. Since large amounts of sensitive patient data have to be used, it is important to discuss data protection and ethical aspects in detail. This increases credibility and addresses potential points of criticism. The exposé mentions data protection as a challenge, but remains at a general level. Concrete measures or strategies to solve data protection problems are not mentioned. Address data protection more specifically, e.g. by using anonymization techniques, data encryption or by observing data protection laws. This shows that ethical concerns are taken seriously and that the project is realistic.
