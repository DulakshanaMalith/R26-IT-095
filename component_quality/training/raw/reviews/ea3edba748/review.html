Motivation

The motivation has the goal to catch reader's interest, explain the specific problem and in which domain it occurs and also how the problem is currently handled. It should also state research questions explicitly and precisely.

Your motivation section is to short. It does not explain the problem and the domain detailed enough.

Approach

This section has the goal to explain current State of the Art (SOTA) which tackles the problem, which critique the SOTA currently faces and also introduce necesary theoretical work on which your approach is based on. It should then clearly state what you want to do to tackle the problem, including challenges and limitations.

You list some papers or theories without explaining them. If you found some good papers about the SOTA and related theoretical work, it would be good to explain them more detailed, how they are connected, what are the limitations of the SOTA.

Your methods are well structured but should be more detailed. When designing the methods, it is useful to think about (and maybe even write down) whoch part of the methods answers which part of the research question. Possible challenges and limitations of the methods should be adressed.
