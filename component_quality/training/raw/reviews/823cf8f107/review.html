Summary

The proposed work is about investigating the efficiency of automatic patient referral using generative AI, in particular the extent to which it is reliable and how much time doctors can save as a result.

Strengths

  • The motivation part in an exposé should give the reader an understanding of exactly what the problem is and why this work is so important. Your hook conveys very well a problem that many readers can identify with, which is very good. To improve the overall picture, make sure that the red line is emphasised even more and think again about the transitions between the paragraphs.
  • The research questions should provide a clear picture of what the goal of the research work is. By subdividing your main research question, you have limited the scope, which is very good. Furthermore, you have addressed the individual RQs with reference in both the approach and the timetable.

Weaknesses

  • The approach serves to show exactly how to answer the research questions in order for the reader to have a clear understanding of your plan. This is where your exposé is weak, as it is not clear what kind of model should be used, what the input and output for the model is. The tendency is towards an automatic call assistant, but it's not really 100% clear. Try reading the text again and consider whether the individual paragraphs are consistent with each other and whether your project is really described in sufficient detail.
  • Also, the state of the art serves to show what has been done so far in the field, which you have done with some examples. Nevertheless, the weaknesses and how your work differs from the existing work are missing. Make this more clear, what are the differences and what is missing in the work presented?
  • The theoretical framework of the exposé serves as a scientific basis and orientation guide for the planned research. In your case, this is missing completely. Take another look at various theoretical theories and consider to what extent one of the theories could be applicable.

Other

The format should be consistent to make it easier for the reader to fulfil their expectations of your text. Take another look at your terminology (e.g. health insurance is not always clear). You can also introduce abbreviations to make it more explicit. There are also weaknesses in the standardised format with regard to capitalisation (e.g. in the timeline) or in the bibliography. For the last point, you should take another look at the structure of a bibtex file.
