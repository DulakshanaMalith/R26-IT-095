The expose is about evaluating the potential of using LLMs to generate exercises for students. It does this by using a mixed-method approach to compare generated exercises to handmade ones.

Strengths:
The research question fills a clear research gap in the area of AI in education.
It also uses proper citations and includes a complete and consistent Bibliography.
Weaknesses
The scope should be realistic and achievable for a bachelor thesis. But it is too great for a bachelor's thesis. This is because the creation of high-quality hand-made exercises is very difficult. The author would need to reserve way more than one week for the creation. He should focus on only one of the sub-research-questions, where it is not as crucial to create as many high-quality hand-made exercises for meaningful results.
There needs to be a clearly stated and described SOTA. In this expose the current SOTA is only very briefly mentioned and not explained. The author should seek to provide more detail on the current SOTA and describe why it's necessary to use his approach of comparing the generated exercises to the human-made ones.
It should be clear in which domains the research is situated. The author doesn't explicitly name the domains. He should name domains like Machine Learning, and AI in education for example.
Other
While the language is generally clear, there are some unnecessarily convoluted sentences, which could be broken up into smaller parts.
