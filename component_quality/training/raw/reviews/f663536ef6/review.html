  • The goal of this expose is to conduct a thorough literature review around the topic of holographic AI assistants, mainly regarding HCI design principles and usability.

Strengths:

  • Your Sub-Research Questions are well defined. You took a small scope, and formulated open questions
  • Your schedule is very thorough
  • You present the important limitation of your methodology (being constrained to secondary sources only) well

Weaknesses:

  • The methodology section should clearly show how you plan to address and answer all of your (sub-)research questions. A literature review makes sense, but you treat all of your research questions the same and stay quite superficial. Think about treating your SRQs individually (even if they get to a similar methodology as a result), as it also shows to the reader you have thought about all SRQs and present how you will tackle them
  • Already the main RQ should have a certain scope such that it can be answered in your thesis. Your RQ is really broad, and cannot possibly be answered fully in a thesis. Take inspiration in your SRQs and narrow down the main RQ already (and possibly not all four SRQs are relevant if the scope becomes too wide)
  • There should be a coherent citation style that fits into the written text. Your references to [1], [2] etc in the middle of the text are usually not good practice. If the latex template doesnt provide you with a different citation style, cite differently and put the references after your sentences, not in the middle
  • The theoretical framework should be correct by itself. "HCI" is not a set of design principles, but the name of a discipline. Become more precise in your language and use citations in this paragraph to assure good scientific writing

Combining different existing work should help the overall structure and motivation of your expose, showing a clear gap in SOTA and justifying the necessity of your research. Your SOTA section includes different sources, but doesn't combine them in a meaningful way. When you write the SOTA section, try to build a comprehensive image for the reader such that it clearly leads towards your research.
