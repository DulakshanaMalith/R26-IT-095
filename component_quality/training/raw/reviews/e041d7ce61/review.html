Summary

This exposé proposes an investigation into the potential improvements and downsides that Generative AI might bring to traditional personalized medicine. Possible advantages include acceleration of drug discovery, enhancement of diagnostic accuracy and support of predictive and preventive care. The main methodology utilized is a critical review of the relevant literature.

Strengths

The large potential of Generative AI to drastically improve traditional personalized healthcare is shown thoroughly and the reader can grasp the high relevance of this research easily. All the promising features of this technology are listed, but also the possible downsides are explained. It is clear, that answering the research question is highly important for the healthcare sector.

Additionally, the methodology is clearly explained and includes possible challenges, that need to be taken care of.

The goal of the study - to summarize the advantages and challenges of integrating Generative AI into personalized healthcare - is also clearly stated and the main research question is apparent.

Weaknesses

The research question of the thesis should be precise and specific, such that a concrete scientific answer can be found. Especially, the research should aim to answer a question that has not already been investigated. The proposed research question might be too broad to fulfill this criteria. Simply investigating the possible advantages and disadvantages of Generative AI in personalized healthcare is research that has already been done by papers included in the bibliography. Instead, a more specific aspect of the field (e.g. "What are the possible privacy concerns when introducing Generative AI into personalized healthcare?" or "What methods are most suited to facilitate further integration of Generative AI into personalized healthcare?" ) should be chosen and researched.

Furthermore, the theoretical framework should establish the guidelines and existing research, which plays a fundamental role in how the question will be answered. Therefore, this section should include the state-of-the-art methods on how to perform literature reviews in order to draw summarized conclusions of the possible advantages and disadvantages. These methods are missing from the actual theoretical framework of this exposé. Including such papers into the theoretical framework would clarify the basis, from which you would like to start your investigation.

Finally, a good schedule acts as a guideline for writing a thesis such that each essential part of the total work is listed and the researcher always knows what currently needs to be done. This is achieved by designating a concrete timespan for each subtask of the entire workload. A finished schedule should also include a section where the results are collected and conclusions are drawn. The schedule of this exposé however lacks these key parts of writing the proposed thesis. Adding a timespan for these tasks would improve the schedule and could lead to a better thesis outcome in the end.
