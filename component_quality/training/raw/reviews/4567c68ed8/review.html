The exposé proposes a thesis developing a holographic AI assistant for customer service which will then be trialed in a user study with the goal of collecting feedback.

The motivation of an expose should point out the reasons why the research is relevant. As the name suggests, it motivates the approach and also needs to convince the reader that the work fills a relevant gap.

Your motivation does a great job at this, with a hook that leads nicely into the anker, pointing out the current issues in the customer service industry.

However, this is only true under the assumption that your claims are correct. While they seem sensible, it would be nice to see some work on these topics being referenced. In order to perfect your well-written motivation, you should back up its claims. See my annotations for places where I feel a reference could be needed.

A research question (RQ) needs to strike a balance between broadness, leaving room for answers, and specificity. Usually, questions which lead to simple Yes/No-answers are not the best at this, as they artificially limit the answer space. They also need to be precise in order to efficiently guide study design and inform the reader about the area of research.

This exposé contains a research question on the broad area it intends to study. However, it is too broad, making it hard to identify how exactly this sub-field of AI will profit and where the focus of the research lies. It also raises concerns about the amount of work to be conducted, as answering it within a single thesis seems infeasible.

You should come up with a narrower research question in order to identify your sub-area of research more closely, which will help you in improving your study design and to better position your research against other works, as it more clearly points out which specific problems in this topic you aim to address instead of the topic as a whole. For this purpose, introducing sub-RQs might be helpful. Consider something like "How do AI assistants transform customer interactions?" as a RQ leading to an area to investigate.

An exposé needs to inform about the work that the bachelor's thesis it is for will entail. This workload needs to be feasible within the time frame of a thesis and not too difficult.

While you propose a lot of things to do in a thesis, it seems to be too much to achieve within the allocated 24 weeks. You seem to plan developing the holographic assistant from scratch and then conducting an extensive user study with it, then using that feedback to improve the original assistant. Since the development of the agent probably contains both building an AI model, training it, and then combining that with the holographic technologies, you should instead consider not doing all of the work yourself. Relying on some existing and proven solutions instead, where applicable, such as considering what existing models might suit your needs and align with your goals instead of building it yourself should bring your planned thesis closer to something that can be completed within the given time frame.

The planned work is also expressed in the schedule. A clear schedule not only helps with keeping track of tasks when carrying out the thesis itself, it also proves in advance that you have a realistic grasp on the time and work the project will take. Writing it forces one to consider all aspects of the project alongside the effort they entail.

Your current schedule is the example schedule given to us in the template. This might count as plagiarism, which is not in the realms of proper scientific conduct. You therefore should design a realistic schedule for your project, which might also help you with splitting up tasks and figuring out how best to deal with the amount of work currently proposed in the exposé.

A specific challenge for your schedule would be making everything fit into the given time frame of 24 weeks while still reserving some buffer space in case something unforseen happens. The reflection on your work will allow your to design a better study, which might in turn help with further improving your RQs.

Another important aspect of the exposé is showing that you know the relevant literature on the topic and are able to conduct academic work. This includes referencing relevant literature and positioning your novel contributions in relation to other works.
