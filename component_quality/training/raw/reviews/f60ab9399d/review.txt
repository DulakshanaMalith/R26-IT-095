Summary

This exposé explores the application of AI assistants in private tutoring, specifically focusing on augmented reality (AR) scenarios. It aims to evaluate the learning experience provided by AI tutors in comparison to traditional private tutors, addressing the impact on educational outcomes.

Strengths
Relevance of the Topic:
The research addresses a significant and timely issue by investigating how AI can assist with educational challenges, particularly in the context of remote learning exacerbated by the COVID-19 pandemic. The focus on AR-enhanced learning environments highlights the innovative potential of this work.
Scope of Research Questions:
The research questions are well-defined and appropriately scoped for a ~6-month thesis. They strike a balance between feasibility and ambition, making them achievable within the given timeframe.
Writing Style:
The exposé is written in clear, concise, and formal language, ensuring accessibility and professionalism. This reflects a solid understanding of academic writing conventions.
State-of-the-Art (SOTA):
The exposé establishes a strong foundation by referencing relevant state-of-the-art studies and situating the proposed research within the existing academic discourse. The inclusion of appropriate citations strengthens the work’s credibility.
Experimental Setup:
The experiments are well-described, detailing essential aspects such as participant selection, control groups, scope, and timeline. This comprehensive approach enhances the feasibility and rigor of the proposed study.
Weaknesses
Typos and Text Formatting:
Minor issues in formatting detract from the readability and polish of the document. For instance:
Listing control groups across multiple lines is inefficient. Using a table or graphical representation to organize this information would improve clarity and presentation.
Schedule:
The schedule is overly brief and lacks detail.
Tasks such as “first test level, creating intractable items” are vague and require further explanation. Including specifics about the timeline, milestones, and deliverables would provide better structure and transparency.
Bibliography:
While the bibliography contains relevant and recent references, it is limited to only five citations. Given the wealth of literature on AI in education, expanding the bibliography to include a broader range of studies would strengthen the theoretical foundation and contextualize the research more comprehensively.

Conclusion

This exposé demonstrates strong potential with its timely and relevant topic, clear writing, and well-conceived experimental design. Addressing minor weaknesses in formatting, schedule detail, and the breadth of references will significantly enhance the overall quality and impact of the work. I look forward to seeing the revised version!
