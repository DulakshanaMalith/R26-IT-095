Weaknesses

However, in this proposal there are some areas where the language could be further refined. For example avoid using sentences like “I want…”, "my", "myself"... Instead, consider make it more neutral, objective. I recommend reviewing the text to eliminate subjective language.

Aim of this lecture is learning how to write the proposal into clear sections. To improve the proposal, restructure it into separate sections for SOTA, Theoretical Framework, and Methodology. This way it will be easier for reader to understand your research easily.

  • (Hook)

The hook introduces the importance of programming and the challenges faced by first-year students, providing relevant context. However, it lacks an engaging or compelling start that would immediately capture the reader's attention, making it feel somewhat general. To improve the hook, consider using a provocative question or striking statistic to draw the reader in and emphasize the urgency of addressing the problem.

  • (Schedule)

The schedule is too general and lacks specific timelines, and connections to the research questions and sub-questions. The schedule should explicitly align with the research questions and sub-questions. For instance, the research question could be divided into specific phases. To improve the schedule, break it down into clear phases.

  • (Methodology)

The methodology lacks sufficient detail regarding how the ITS agent will be developed and evaluated. Include more specifics on the implementation steps, data processing, and evaluation metrics. Elaborate on the design and testing process of the ITS agent to enhance methodological clarity.
