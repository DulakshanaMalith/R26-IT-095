The exposé includes all the important components, although the sections on the State of the Art (SOTA) and Theoretical Framework (TF) are somewhat brief and could be expanded for better coverage. The relevance of the problem is generally well explained, especially concerning the risks of AI. However, the discussion on risks predominantly focuses on the "generation of incorrect information." Given the significance of this issue, providing 3-4 specific examples of risks associated with AI would strengthen the argument.

Gaps in the existing literature are identified, demonstrating that critical aspects are missing in current studies. The research questions align with the problem statement but are quite general. This generality could lead to varying outcomes, particularly when considering different patient groups (e.g., cancer patients) or geographic locations. The study's location is an important factor and is not mentioned, which could lead to significant differences in outcomes based on cultural and systemic contexts.

Theories and concepts are introduced at a very high level. Including a concrete example of an AI tool currently in use would provide better context and improve comprehension. The choice of methodology is justified based on the research questions, and the author outlines how the methods will answer them. However, the concept of a semi-structured interview is not explained, leaving a gap in understanding.

While the risks and limitations of the topic are well explained, the work itself faces challenges that are not fully addressed, such as identifying appropriate patients for interviews. The schedule appears realistic and achievable, though minor adjustments could improve feasibility. For instance, the one week allocated for interviews might be insufficient, while the two weeks planned for data analysis could be shortened to one week, allowing more time for conducting interviews.

The overall argumentation is convincing and clearly states the goals of the research. However, the research question feels somewhat scattered within the motivation chapter, and consolidating it in one place could enhance clarity. Providing additional examples of tools currently in use would also enhance understanding, although illustrations are not deemed necessary at this stage.

Finally, the exposé does not specify where the study will be conducted. This is an important detail, as outcomes could vary significantly between regions, such as Germany and the USA. Addressing this omission would provide a clearer framework for the study.

In general, the exposé demonstrates strong progress and solid groundwork. Many of the points raised here likely stem from the fact that the work is not yet finalized. As the research develops further, these aspects can be refined and expanded to create a comprehensive and compelling study.
