Summary:

The exposé investigates the extent to which Large Language Models (LLMs) can provide personalised, formative feedback on programming tasks that takes into account both functional correctness and code quality. The aim is to analyse the accuracy and perception of the LLM-generated feedback and to evaluate its influence on improvements in student code.
