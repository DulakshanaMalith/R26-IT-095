Summary
This exposé explores how LLM-powered tools can transform education by making personalised learning more scalable, effective, and accessible. The current education system struggles to provide personalised learning at scale, with traditional classrooms and existing online platforms often failing to adapt to individual student needs. Although online learning is rapidly expanding, many solutions remain inflexible. Recent advancements in LLMs like GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 offer a promising path forward. These models enable the creation of adaptive teaching agents that can deliver real-time, personalized support across various subjects.

Strengthes
Few linguistic errors; overall clear and comprehensible language 
Preliminary title fits the topic well and shows creativity.
SOTA is included and cited correctly.

Weakness
The introduction lacks a compelling hook to capture the reader’s interest. Starting with general statements rather than a striking fact, question, or statistic with citations.
Using the comparison between traditional vs. online education as a motivation for the study is problematic. The topic is about LLM teaching agents, which should be compared with other AI/intelligent systems which have similar functions.
The research questions are not precise enough. For example, in RQ1, the author mentioned how to optimise LLM in different subject areas. In RQ3, the author mentioned how the LLM can enhance the learning outcomes in various educational settings. The differences between different subject areas and educational settings are not discussed in the exposé
Citation format is not consistent. For instance, citations [1] and [5] use different formats.
