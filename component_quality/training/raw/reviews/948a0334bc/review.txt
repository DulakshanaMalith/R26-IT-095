Summary:
This expose discusses the issue of language filtering in LLMs preventing the diagnoses of MDD (Major depressive disorder) in users/patients. The student highlighted that, while LLMs are useful in assisting mental health professionals, LLMs have shown lower performance in detecting MDD compared to other mental health disorders. For this reason, the student aims to investigate how language filters affect the detection of MDD and what can be changed in these filtering policies to improve MDD diagnosis while maintaining ethical safeguards in place.
