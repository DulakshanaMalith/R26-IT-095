Summary:

The exposé examines the data protection problems that arise when using generating AI to create personalised treatment plans and analyses how technical and organisational measures can mitigate these risks. The aim of the study is to identify data protection requirements as well as specific risks and to develop solution strategies using qualitative methods such as document analysis, case studies and expert interviews.

Strength:

  • The exposé is well-structured and easy to follow, which provides a clear narrative for the reader. The logical flow of this work ensures that the core research questions, methods and objectives are understandable and well connected.
  • The examples used in the text are well chosen and effectively support the argumentation. They help to contextualize the problem for the reader, demonstrating why it is critical to address this specific challenge.
  • The sub-questions are particularly strong. They build upon each other logically, creating a coherent structure for the research. This ensures that the research progresses step by step, covering both theoretical and practical aspects of the problem.
  • The methodology is well explained overall, with clear references to the type of analysis being conducted. The inclusion of examples of interview partners adds specifity to the approach, enhancing the credibility.

Weakness:

  • It is very important to make it clear in an exposé what the aim of the work is in order to give the reader a precise understanding of the objectives. While the main research question is strong and relevant, it is not fully integrated into the text. A smooth transition leading up to the research question is missing. By creating this bridge to the research question, you can strengthen the overall motivation for your study and make the text flow more cohesively.
  • The motivation should explain to the reader why the chosen topic is relevant and which weakness the thesis wants to address. The section is clear but could be explained in more detail. While you mention the rapid advancements in AI and data privacy risks, you could emphasize why this issue is particularly critical in healthcare. For example: Why is this question relevant in the context of healthcare? What are the consequences of failing to address these risks? By answering these questions, you underline why the topic is relevant and highlight the implications of your research. Additionally, you clarify the research gap, which would further strengthen your justification.
  • The privacy risks are mentioned in the text, but they should be explained more in detail. This is important to give the reader an understanding of the relevance of the topic. Additionally, you could go into more detail about the gap you want to fill with your research.
  • The methodology describes how the individual research questions are answered. While the methods are generally well described, sub-RQ 1.2 remains somewhat superficial. Adding details and a clear focus of the analysation would clarify the methodology and make it more concrete. Additionally, it is very important to address potential limitations and practical hurdles of your methods. Acknowledging these limitations strengthens the rigor of your methodology and demonstrates critical reflection. Read your exposé again carefully and fill in the missing details.
  • The time schedule is used to plan the thesis in advance to see whether it is possible in the short time available. The table is too small and should be clearly readable. It is also inconsistent as it switches to German, which breaks the flow of the document. Additionally, week 15-16 is missing entirely, leaving the schedule incomplete. Also, the writing process is only partially included. Adjust the format and language of your timetable and add the missing points.
