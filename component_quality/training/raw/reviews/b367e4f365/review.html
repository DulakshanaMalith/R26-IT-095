Summary:

  • In this study, an open-source LLM shall be integrated into a simple interface for teachers to facilitate generating personalized worksheets and learning content for students

Strengths:

  • You have a very concise and clear writing style
  • You point out well why the problem is important and how it has not been addressed properly yet
  • Your RQs are good!

Weaknesses:

  • Correct citation style is extremely important in academia. In several cases, you do not use references (e.g. "Existing tools...", "Not many such tools for teachers exist", "Most LLM-based solutions...", "principles of HCI") where they are absolutely vital. When in doubt - cite. One of the most important rules in scientific writing!
  • The SOTA section should be used to present similar or related, relevant work, to give context and justification for your research. Your SOTA section is lacking all sorts of relevant, current work. Even if it no solution exists to specifically address your problem, the SOTA section should contain similar approaches, similar challenges (like LLMs in personalized teaching, the "not many tools for teachers", tools for students etc).
  • The methodology section should clearly show how you plan to answer your RQs. In your case, especially the Sub-RQs remain unanswered and it is not clear why you chose your methods, and you contain several imprecisions (what exactly are your metrics, how/where do you collect the requirements, how exactly do you plan your surveys/studies). Always keep the overall structure of the expose in mind when coming up with a methodology. It may help to specifically reference your Sub-RQs to show how you plan to tackle and answer them.
