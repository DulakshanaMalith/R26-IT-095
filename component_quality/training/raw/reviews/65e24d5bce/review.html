Summary:

  • The goal is to develop and evaluate a generative AI model with regards to accuracy, explainability and text response quality for predicting successful individual antidepressant treatments for people with MDD.

Strengths:

  • Your overall style of scientific writing is really good. You combine existing material very well, presenting limitations in the state of the art as well as promising developments.
  • The motivation section is very strong and extensive without repeating yourself, while at the same time giving motivation both for the general problem (antidepressant treatments) as well as the technical problem (lack of trust due to a lack of explainability e.g.)
  • You scope your (Sub-)RQs very well
  • You present a good overview over the SOTA methods and their limitations
  • Your methodology seems suitable to answer your RQs, and you provide a high level of detail

Weaknesses:

  • As a transition between the state of the art and the methodology, a theoretical framework is important to motivate your specific method. In your case, that would probably either be explaining some models/concepts a bit more in detail, or to explain your idea of a model more in depth as "GenAI model" is still quite general. You could e.g. motivate an idea/draft of an architecture, based on existing models or - if you are unsure - explain advantages and disadvantages of different models for this application.
  • Minor weakness: In machine learning, the evaluation of a model is really important which requires a good selection of metrics. Your idea of metrics seem really good, but not yet very precise - e.g. regarding the explainability, or the accuracy/correctness metrics. You can have a look what metrics are frequently used in related work, select suitable specific metrics and also explain why.

Others:

  • You have a couple of commas that are not necessary. But this is now really a minor point.

Generally: Great expose, well done!
