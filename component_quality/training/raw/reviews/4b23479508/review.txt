Summary

This exposé explores the potential benefits and design considerations of a holographic AI assistant presented through virtual reality (VR). While AI chatbots like ChatGPT are widely known, they are not yet integrated into daily routines. The author suggests that a more engaging, graphical user interface (GUI)—specifically a VR-based, holographic assistant—might improve user interaction and adoption.

Strengths

The exposé introduces the link between the rise of generative AI and VR, with potential improvements to AI assistant usability.
The exposé includes a bunch of examples and evidence about the development of generative AI and relevant research
The exposé includes details of the experiment implementation

Weakness
Language Quality: While generally readable, the exposé contains occasional grammatical issues (e.g. tense shifts, minor punctuation issues, awkward phrasing). These do not obscure meaning but detract from the overall polish.
Theoretical Framework: Although the exposé references relevant concepts (like the uncanny valley), it lacks a clearly structured and explicitly stated theoretical framework.
Target Group: The methodology does not clearly define the target participant group beyond a general desire for diversity. More specific criteria would help clarify the scope and impact of the study.
Completeness: While many steps are described in detail, not all aspects of the research question(s) are explicitly tied back to the study design.
