This exposé examines secondary school students' ability to detect errors in ChatGPT-generated mathematical responses. The study explores the influence of age, mathematical ability, and awareness of ChatGPT’s limitations on students' capacity to identify inaccuracies. The methodology combines three structured tests and a survey to analyze these dimensions, guided by theoretical concepts like the Lazy User Theory. The results aim to enhance students' critical thinking and inform educational strategies for integrating AI responsibly into education.
