Summary

You want to use Gen AI to buuild a model of a human which could then be used for health treatments.

Motivation

The motivation has the goal to catch reader's interest, explain specific problem and in which domain it occurs and also how the problem is currently handled. It should also state research questions explicitly and precisely.

Your hook is very good.

After this, you focus a lot about what your proposed system should do. However, it would be important to elaborate further on the problem you want to tackle.

The RQs are good in general, only the first seems like the main RQ.

Approach

In the approach, the goal is to explain current State of the Art and explain the research gap that would further improve the existing work. It should also state your methods, how they relate to your RQs and what their challenges and limitations are.

You do not really write about the SOTA. The SOTA should explain existing technology. If no technology exists which match to your work, you would explain systems that at least use Gen AI in healthcare.

Also, in the theoretical framework, you should write about other work, e.g. theoretical foundation of Gen AI, especially in medicine.

In your methods you should include potential challenges and limitations.

Also, I would consider if you might need more groups to have a comparison for the Gen AI scenario. Otherwise you only have data on the use of Gen AI but you don't know how it performes compared to other systems and approaches.

It would also be useful to get a more detailed explanation about what the Digital Twin actually is (e.g. which type of data)
