Summary

The management of Type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) is complicated by the need to balance clinical guidelines with individual patient needs. Poor medication adherence leads to suboptimal outcomes. This thesis explores how large language models (LLMs) can leverage patient lifestyle data and medical history to enhance personalized medication planning. It addresses gaps in adherence and proposes methods to optimize LLM recommendations for safe and effective treatment.
