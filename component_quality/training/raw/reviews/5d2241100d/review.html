Teaching Mathematics with AI - Adressing Oportunities and Challenges in Modern Education.

Summary

This exposé addresses a very interesting topic and is grounded in a solid theoretical framework. However, the research question and its sub-questions are quite broad and should be refined for clarity. Additionally, the methodology needs to be more detailed to clearly demonstrate how it will address the research question.

Poject proposal

The project aims to explore the potential of AI in assisting teachers and students aged 16 to 19 in mathematics. Based on the results of interviews with teachers, the plan is to develop an AI model that assesses students' strengths and weaknesses and provides personalized lessons and homework assignments. This software will be utilized by 10 students, who will provide feedback both during the trial and at its conclusion.

Strengths

The project features a compelling hook, supported by statistics that are relevant both globally and particularly in Germany. It is also well anchored, as the investigation focuses on mathematics in middle schools—a notable gap, given that most studies concentrate on high school education. This distinction is further emphasized in the state of the art (SOTA) section, where previous studies are referenced. In the theoretical framework, concepts such as constructivism, perceived usefulness, and perceived ease of use are succinctly yet effectively explained, highlighting their significance to the investigation. The challenges faced are also thoroughly addressed. Finally, the schedule is very detailed, including an early contact to schools to recruit participants.

Weacknesses

Research question

The research question should provide the study with a clear goal and direction, while also delineating the boundaries of what will not be investigated. It needs to be clear, relevant, and realistic. Currently, your question is overly broad and cannot be effectively addressed in a single investigation. You might consider focusing specifically on either teachers or students, for instance. Additionally, the sub-questions are too varied; it would be more effective to concentrate on two that are closely related. For example, sub-questions 2 and 3 address how AI can assist teachers in understanding how well students are assimilating content—whether they comprehend it or require further explanation or practice.

Methodology

The methodology should outline how you plan to seek answers to all the research questions. However, given that the questions are numerous and diverse, the proposed methodology may not be sufficient to address them all. For instance, to investigate how students' attitudes toward AI-assisted tools influence the learning process, you should first identify the existing attitudes, then select students who exhibit these attitudes,they should work with AI-assisted tools, and finally messure and compare their evolution. While this approach could potentially address sub-questions 2 and 3, it requires further clarification. Specifically, how will the feedback you mentioned be implemented? Additionally, what methods will be used for data collection?

Others

In scientific writing, it is advisable to avoid the use of the first person (I or we). Your article contains several instances of the first-person perspective. Instead, aim for an objective description of your points, primarily using the passive voice.
