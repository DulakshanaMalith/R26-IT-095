Summary:                   

The proposal aims to develop a holographic AI assistant that bridges the gap between human-like interaction and digital intelligence in augmented reality by leveraging spatial awareness, gesture-based communication, and user-centric design.

Strengths

The methodology integrates cutting-edge tools like Unity3D, ARCore, and AI frameworks, ensuring a state-of-the-art approach.

The proposal follows a logical flow

Weaknesses

  • (Citations)

In scientific writing, one of the most important things is citing the claims and ideas to provide evidence, demonstrate engagement, give credit to the work of other researchers and make your own work more credible for readers. The proposal lacks of citations to support the claims made. For instance, there is no citations in your motivation, which would support your hook or anchor, as well as the SOTA and theoretical framework. Without citations, the points you raise come across as opinions rather than evidence-based insights, which can weaken the academic value of your work. The proposal schould be supported with more scientific research.

  • (Language)

The language in scientific work should be precise, formal, and objective to communicate ideas clearly and credibly, without trying the reader to convince. However, in this proposal there are some areas where the language could be further refined. For example avoid using “we”, “our” in your motivation and approach. Instead, consider make it more neutral, objective. I recommend reviewing the text to eliminate subjective language.

  • (SOTA)

The State-of-the-Art section does not explain whisch AR systems areavailable nad how they work. While the general reference to AR systems is helpful, the SOTA section would benefit from a more in-depth review of recent studies and relevant technologies in AR systems. To strengthen this section, consider including specific examples of existing applications, how they work and their limitations and cite them for more credibility as mentioned before.

  • (Theoretical Framework)

The theoretical framework mentions key concepts such as spatial awareness, non-verbal communication, and social presence, which are crucial for designing a holographic AI assistant. However, the framework lacks clarity in demonstrating how these are directly connected to the research questions and methodological approach, making the argumentation less robust. To strengthen the proposal, the framework should explicitly link these theoretical concepts to the research goals.
