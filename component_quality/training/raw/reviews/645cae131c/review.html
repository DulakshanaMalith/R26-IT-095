Summary:

The proposal examines the effectiveness of LLM-based teaching agents in enhancing student engagement in STEM education, focusing on interactive features and their implementation strategies.

Strength

  1. Important claims are referenced, which gives the proposal a good credibility
  2. Overall logical and well-structured.

Weaknesses

  • (SOTA)

The State-of-the-Art section briefly touches on the role of AI-powered teaching agents. While the general reference to AI tools is helpful, the SOTA section would benefit from a more in-depth review of recent studies and relevant technologies in AI-assisted education in STEM subjects. To strengthen this section, consider including specific examples of existing LLM applications or AI agents used in education, especially those applied to STEM disciplines.

  • (Methodology)

The methodology section provides a general overview, but it lacks sufficient detail on how the study will be executed. More specifics are needed. For example, the quantitative study mentions using "activity logs," "survey-based engagement scales," and "performance tasks," but it does not explain how these tools will be designed, what specific metrics will be tracked, or how data from these sources will be analyzed. It is recommended to give more details.

  • (Schedule)

The schedule is too general and lacks specific timelines, and connections to the research questions and sub-questions. The schedule should explicitly align with the research questions and sub-questions. For instance, the research question could be divided into specific phases. To improve the schedule, break it down into clear phases.

  • (Theoretical Framework)

Theoretical Framework lacks how the results might influence current teaching practices in STEM education. A more explicit connection between the theoretical framework and potential real-world applications in STEM teaching could enhance the relevance of the study. The proposal should link the theoretical findings to practical teaching strategies.
