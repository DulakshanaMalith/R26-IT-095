Feedback
Motivation Section
You’ve laid a strong foundation by introducing holographic AI as a transformative technology in education, but the motivation section could benefit from more refinement. The transition from LLMs to holographic AI feels abrupt and lacks sufficient context, which might confuse readers. Instead, start by establishing the importance of personalized learning and how current educational challenges, like teacher shortages, make the case for AI-driven solutions. Then, gradually introduce holographic AI as the next step and emphasize its unique capabilities. To make this section more engaging, include specific examples or scenarios where holographic AI could solve existing issues or enhance the learning experience. This will make your argument clearer and more compelling. Avoid discussing interdisciplinarity here; instead, keep the focus on the relevance and potential impact of holographic AI in education.
Research Questions
Your main RQ is concise and provides a solid starting point, but the sub-RQs need more precision. For Sub-RQ 2.1, you should narrow your focus to a single area of education, as trying to address all areas would stretch the scope of a bachelor’s thesis. Sub-RQ 2.2 is a strong question, but rephrasing it for clarity would improve readability.
State of the Art (SOTA)

Additionally, including examples of existing studies and their limitations would give your analysis more depth. For instance, you could highlight studies that focus on categorization or prediction tasks but fail to address interactivity or scalability in educational contexts. This would clearly position your research as addressing these shortcomings.
