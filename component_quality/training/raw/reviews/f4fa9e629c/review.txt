Summary

The exposé outlines a research project exploring the opportunities and challenges of using holographic AI agents in music education. Through a mixed-methods approach involving interviews and questionnaires, it aims to understand the perceptions of teachers, learners, and parents across different educational settings. 

Strengths

Relevance and Novelty of Topic:
The exposé addresses an innovative and underexplored topic by integrating holographic AI agents into music education. The motivation for applying this technology in an area traditionally dependent on physical interaction is compelling and grounded in practical challenges like accessibility and cost.
Balanced Methodology:
The mixed-methods approach, combining interviews, questionnaires, and theoretical insights, ensures a comprehensive analysis. The inclusion of diverse participant groups and the focus on specific educational contexts add depth and validity to the proposed study.
Integration of State-of-the-Art Research:
The exposé effectively situates the study within the existing literature, identifying gaps such as the lack of focus on teachers and parents. References to recent advancements in holographic AI agent design strengthen the theoretical foundation.
Practical Considerations:
The detailed schedule, with early participant acquisition and buffer time, demonstrates an understanding of potential challenges in data collection. The mention of tools like automatic transcription reflects thoughtful planning to manage time effectively.

Weaknesses and Suggestions for Improvement

Clear Research Focus:
The exposé should focus on a few specific, well-defined research questions, and describe experiments that tackle these questions in great detail. The research question and subquestions are stated but are formulated as very broad and general questions. Subsequently, the approach chapter does not contain a detailed enough description of the experimental setup. The exposé would improve a lot by narrowing the focus and giving more details about the planned implementation..
Theoretical Framework:
While the exposé references relevant literature, the theoretical framework lacks detail on how it will inform empirical instrument development. You should elaborate on how specific design principles or characteristics of holographic AI agents from the literature will shape the interview questions and questionnaires.
Ethical Considerations:
The exposé does not address ethical issues related to the use of holographic AI agents in education, such as data privacy, potential biases, or the impact on teacher employment. I suggest including a brief discussion on how ethical concerns will be managed in the study and addressed in the research findings.
Literature Review Gaps:
Although the exposé highlights gaps in the literature, it could better justify why these gaps are significant for the broader adoption of holographic AI agents. Also, the exposé does not reference many publications, and the bibliography has some inconsistencies. Fixing these issues and broadening the spectrum of literature review would strengthen the exposé significantly.

With adjustments to the scope and additional refinement of the methodology, this exposé has the potential to be very good. I am looking forward to the final iteration!
