Peer Review

Your exposé on "Enhancing Programming Education: Evaluating the Effectiveness of ITS Agents for First-Year and Advanced Students" is well structured and you do a good job at catching the readers attention. The papers you researched fit your topic well and your improvements of their designs seem beneficial, repeating studies with a larger sample size or with improved methodolagy are usually a good approach in order to build upon existing research. Your plan to use a newer version (GPT4o) of the same model (GPT3.5/4) used in older studies is also good and an obvious choice to further evaluate the topic. 
While the structure and content of your text are good and your study design seems well thought out you should look into methods to improve the language of your text. Make sure to use a grammar and spelling checking tool as there are a few repeating spelling and grammar mistakes. You also tend to use short sentences, this can be good for readability but hinders the flow when reading your text. Try to bring some of your sentences together, this will make your text easier to read. 
You mention Python as an easy language to learn, you could elaborate on that for readers that are not familiar with programming languages. 
Regarding your method I think it would be better to use a broader scale than 1-3 for your evaluation. You could look into existing grading systems regarding the helpfulness of instructions. Looking into psychology research on education might be of interest to find a suiting scale. In studies like yours it is common to use existing scales or scales from prior experiments as this improves the comparability of different studies. 
I think the schedule you provided is reasonable. You could incorporate more sub steps on writing in order to make sure you stay on top of tracking your progress and start writing at an early time.
Overall your exposé has many promising aspects, the most important point is to improve the language.
