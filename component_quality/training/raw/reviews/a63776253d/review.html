This proposal is showing how AI can be integrated into mathematics education to tackle the problem of declining student performance and teacher shortages in Germany. The proposal is describing an idea to develop a tailored AI platform to enhance mathematical skills and includes considerations for system design, implementation, and ethical issues.

Strengths:

The document has very good readability due to the good structure and decent use of language, making it easy to understand the text.

It has a great theoretical foundation, which adds depth to the concept.

The research questions (RQ's) are precise and feel realistic to be answered in a bachelor thesis (1 Motivation).

In general, I like the extra effort and detail put into this proposal, such as the use of a custom table and the graph in the beginning (1.1 Motivation).

Weaknesses:

The testing phases 1 and 2 (2.0.3 Methodology) might not be enough. I would consider adding another week there and possibly using some of the buffer time.

The ethical challenges are only mentioned on the surface and could benefit from more elaboration (2.0.5 Difficulties), for example adding topics like the bias and data privacy problems.

Another point would be link some already successful projects to your proposal so your project seems even more realistic to achieve (1 Motivation).

Others:

For me, it is confusing that you are not using week numbers (3 Schedule), but instead use "Week 1-4" for every month.

Some of the links you offered do not work when copied and pasted, like those from "doi.org" (3.4).

The language and structure of your text is clear and professional.

This proposal also has a useful visual and a good bibliography.
