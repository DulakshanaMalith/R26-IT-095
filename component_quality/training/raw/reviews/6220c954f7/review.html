Summary

This exposé investigates the application of holographic AI assistants in mental health care, focusing on their potential to alleviate the growing demand for professional help with issues such as depression and loneliness. The work aims to explore the feasibility and impact of such technologies in a psychological setting.

Strengths

  • Relevance of the Topic:
    • The exposé addresses a critical and timely societal issue by exploring how AI technology can assist with mental health challenges. Its focus on depression and loneliness highlights the importance of the research.
  • Writing Style:
    • The language is clear, concise, and formal, making the exposé easy to read and understand. This demonstrates good academic writing skills and professionalism.
  • State-of-the-Art (SOTA):
    • The exposé provides a solid foundation by referencing the relevant SOTA, including appropriate citations. This situates the proposed research within the current academic discourse.
  • Experimental Setup:
    • The experiments are described in impressive detail, covering critical elements such as participant selection, scope, and time frame. This clarity provides confidence in the feasibility and thoroughness of the proposed study.
  • Schedule and Bibliography:
    • The schedule includes realistic and well-defined milestones, ensuring that the thesis is time-managed effectively. The bibliography, with 11 relevant references, demonstrates thorough preparation and grounding in the literature.

Weaknesses

  • Scope of Research Questions:
    • Although the research questions are stated and well-organized, the overall scope is overly ambitious for a ~6-month thesis project:
      • Incorporating advanced holographic technologies is inherently complex and may exceed the time and resource constraints of the thesis.
      • Exploring ethical implications alongside technical and practical aspects is a substantial undertaking that could warrant its own thesis.
    • Refining the scope by focusing on a narrower aspect—such as the technical feasibility, user interaction, or a specific demographic—would enhance the project's manageability and depth.

Conclusion:

This exposé is well-written and thoughtfully structured, demonstrating strong academic preparation and a clear vision. By narrowing the scope and addressing the outlined challenges, the thesis can become both feasible and impactful. I look forward to the revised version!
