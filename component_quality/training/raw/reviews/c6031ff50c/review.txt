Summary
Your Exposé suggests a study to investigate the potential of LLMs/ChatGPT in learning latin.
Through this, latin lessons could be personalized and help students to better learn the language.
You want to study how the recent improvements in LLMs affects their capability to understand and translate latin. 
Furthermore you plan to develop a Latin AI-Learning Assistant and compare how students accomplish tasks with and without this system.



It is good that you try to cover various aspects of LLMs as Latin tutors. However, your methods could be more aligned to them.
You write that you want to focus on LLMs as a teacher. However it seems that only the LLMs capabilittes to give valuable hints is studied. Other tasks of tutors, like explaining rules and grammar, answering student's questions and creating tasks seem unadressed by your study. Also they don't get compared with classical teachers and textbooks (RQ3). The range of ethical concerns seems not adress by your method. 
On the other hand, you want to examine how ChatGPT performs compared to a student, which is not mentioned in the research questions. 
Maybe it could be a good idea to revisit the research questions and think about how each of them will be answered, such that they will all be covered. 

The link to your ChatGPT conversation is a good way of demonstrating its current Latin abilitites. To improve the experience, you could do a new conversation, and give the meta instruction in an initial prompt to make the conversation more fluent. 

It is good that you use common abbreviations (like LLM), but try to introduce them only once in the beginning.

There are grammar and spelling errors. It could be a good idea to use grmmar checking tools to make sure everything is correct.
