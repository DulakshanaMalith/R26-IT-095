Summary

This exposé provides a detailed exploration of the potential and challenges of large language model (LLM)-based teaching agents in personalized education. The thesis aims to identify features of LLM-based teaching agents, and how they can be extracted from LLM models.

Strengths
Clear Motivation: 
The introduction effectively establishes the need for personalized education and how LLM-based teaching agents can address this gap, citing relevant literature to support its claims.
Methodological Rigor: 
The proposed mixed-method approach, combining probing techniques with insights from educational literature, is appropriate for investigating the internal representations of teaching agents.
Feasibility and Planning: 
The timeline is well-structured, with clear milestones and buffers to address potential delays.

Weaknesses
Focused Research Question: 
The exposé should contain specific research questions of appropriate scope, and a methodology that addresses these questions. The proposal identifies a critical research gap, but does not define the scope and focus of the thesis. The research questions (with potential sub questions) should be clearly stated in the motivation.
Complexity for a Bachelor Thesis: 
The proposed scope, which includes designing probing methods, implementing them, and analyzing results, may be overly ambitious for a bachelor's level project. The exposé acknowledges this but could benefit from more explicit suggestions for scaling down if needed.
Limited Focus on Ethical Concerns: 
While biases in training datasets are mentioned, the exposé does not adequately address the ethical implications of deploying LLM-based teaching agents, such as fairness, data privacy, and accountability.
Structure of the approach section: 
The exposé should be easy to read, and should contain appropriate formatting to help with readability. The approach section does not contain any headers or subsections, which makes it difficult to follow. A more detailed structure would improve this work a lot.
Language and Grammar: 
The exposé should be written in formal language, with easy-to-read sentences. The whole exposé would benefit from a thorough proof-reading, as it contains many complex sentences and typos.
Bibliography
The references should follow a unified citation style, and contain all necessary information of the cited articles. This exposé misses key information of several publications, like publication venue. Also, the citation style of some publications varies significantly.

Conclusion

Overall, the exposé demonstrates a solid foundation and a meaningful research direction. With a clearer focus, more concrete methodological details, and some structural improvements, it has the potential to be a very good exposé. I am looking forward to the final version!
