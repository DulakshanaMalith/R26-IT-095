Summary:

The aim of this work is to measure how well current LLMs can assist in language learning at different levels of difficulty, and how different input methods may differ in providing help for a given level of difficulty.

Strength:

  • It is very important to make it clear in an exposé what the aim of the work is in order to give the reader a precise understanding of the objectives. This was achieved very well in this exposé through the well-formulated research questions.

Weakness:

  • In scientific papers, it is very important to avoid errors in spelling and grammar. Your work contains some grammatical errors (e.g. "neigher - not") and repetitions (e.g. "language language", "personalised" - "individual"). To improve the linguistic quality of your exposé, you should read through your exposé carefully again. You can also run various spell checkers, e.g. using Word.

Within your exposé, the problem is not sufficiently explained to convey the relevance. Think about why you find this topic relevant and reread your motivation. What consequences does this problem have for certain groups of people or the society? Supplement this with examples and facts.
  • The state-of-the-art should position your work against existing reasearch. In particular, you should show which gaps or oppertunities currently exist that can be addressed. Your SOTA is a little too superficial. Supplement this with further information on the relevance and current weaknesses of SOTA. In addition, explain how your work differentiates itself from the current SOTA.
  • Within the theoretical framework, the theories used in the thesis are presented and their relevance justified. Your theoretical framework has a very good structure. However, you could explain it in more detail. Read through it carefully and supplement it with further information.
  • The methodology describes how the individual research questions are to be answered. The methodology in your exposé should be described in more detail. Read through your work again and supplement it with further explanations and examples. Think about how or where you could recruit the students/teachers and what the qualitative survey should look like.
