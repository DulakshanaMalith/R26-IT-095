Strengths:

  • Very good structure of your expose! It is easy to follow, but not informally written.
  • The methodology is very well written with a good attention for details, and it is great how you tailor it towards every SRQ (and reference it)
  • The theoretical framework fits in well.
  • Good overview over the SOTA

Weaknesses:

  • It is very important in academia to provide correct citations/references, not only in the SOTA section. In your motivation section, you refer to current/recent work without referencing the correct material. When going through your motivation again, think for every sentence whether it refers to existing work or not, and if yes, don't hesitate to use references several times if necessary.
  • Not only case studies and interviews, but also the development of a prototype is scientific work. Both in the SOTA and in the methodology, this should be reflected by providing more details than right now. Specifically with the bias-mitigation techniques being mentioned as one of the main parts of your research and with your theoretical framework being integrated into the teaching agent, one would expect more in-depth information about the development as well. Note that you don't have to come up with specific ideas, it may suffice to present superficially some current work that you might use, and then explain how you could use it.
