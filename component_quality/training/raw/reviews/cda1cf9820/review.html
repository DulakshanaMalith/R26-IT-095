Summary:

This project aims to investigate how content filtering built into LLMs could affect their ability to diagnose major depressive disorder. It points out that LLMs have the ability to analyze large amounts of text, but content filtering could block user's suicidal thoughts from being handled and analyzed by the system, therefore reducing its accuracy in diagnosing MDD. To investigate the effect, the author aims to use a mixed approach of literature analysis, experiments, and quantitative analysis.
