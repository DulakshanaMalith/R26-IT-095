Summary:

  • Flaws in the medical/pharmaceutical system like incorrect or poorly coordinated medications, slow and inefficient drug development or antiobiotic resistances, not enough specialized medicine etc could be improved upon by using the capabilities of Generative AI. This study aims at comparing the SOTA with traditional methods, and focuses on overcoming practical challenges like the lack of standards, ethical and legal problems.

Strengths:

  • The exposee is very well structured and readable. Many good transitions and justifications for methods and claims are made
  • The goals and research questions are very clear and narrow enough for a thesis
  • Different literature is combined in a nice way to convince the reader about the necessity and potential of this study, explaining current challenges and limitations

Weaknesses:

  • It is vital for the structure of the exposee that the research questions are evident and clear. You split the research questions and don't have a place where you state the complete research questions (plus sub RQs). Bundle them together in one spot and refer to them later for even better structure.
  • In order to compare different methods, metrics should be defined in the methodology section. Other than "accuracy and speed", you stay quite vague. Clarify how you want to measure the accuracy and speed (and potential other metrics) which leads to answering your RQ

When presenting the SOTA, context has to be given by you. In the part about different Generative AI models like GANs or VAEs, or even Reinforcement learning, you merely describe different methods without giving any context about them. Try to summarize the key findings when describing them just like you did in the previous paragraphs.
