Summary:

The thesis aims to investigate the use of LLM-based teaching assistants in education, in particular their potential for personalised learning. For this purporse, a mixed-methods analysis will be conducted using both qualitative interviews and quantitative surveys with students to assess their experiences and the effectiveness of LLM-based teaching assistants in preparing them for exams.

Strength:

  • A well-organised exposé is crucial for clarity and readability. Your exposé has a very good structure that allows the reader to follow the flow easily.
  • The state of the art should present your work in comparison to existing research. You have done this very well in your exposé. The inclusion of current studies and approaches supports this even more.
  • The time schedule is used to plan the thesis in advance to see whether it is possible in the short time available. Your schedule has a very clear structure with realistic time targets.

Weakness:

  • The motivation should explain to the reader why the chosen topic is relevant and which weakness the thesis wants to address. Your motivation has a very good structure, but could be more detailed. Try to answer the following questions with your text. What is the precise problem that you want to address? What are the consequences of the problem and why is it relevant? Also research how the problem is currently being dealt with.
  • In scientific papers it is very important to include current studies to strengthen the credibility and relevance of the work. At some points in your exposé, references to your information are missing. You should add these. In addition, you only list what the papers you used investigated without explaining their context and relevance to the research question. Look at the papers again and summarize their core message. Think about why this paper is relevant to your exposé.
  • It is very important to make it clear in an exposé what the aim of the work is in order to give the reader a precise understanding of the objectives. There is a research question missing from your exposé. Think about which question you want to answer with your research. It is also helpful to divide the main question into several sub-questions in order to keep a better overview.
  • The title serves to indicate the subject area of the exposé and to encourage the reader to read it. You should come up with a more creative title that differs from the one suggested in the lecture. Think of a better title. To find inspiration, you can look at other works/articles on this topic on the internet.
  • The time schedule is used to plan the thesis in advance to see whether it is possible in the short time available. It is therefore very important that all aspects of the thesis are included. The writing of the thesis is missing in your time schedule. Think about which chapters all belong in a thesis and when they should best be written. Also add more details to your description.
