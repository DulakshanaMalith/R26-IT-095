Summary:

  • This study aims at how personalized AI systems can be used to increase mathematical skills of students by tailoring an AI platform, and conducting interviews with both students and teachers for requirements and feedback.

Strengths:

  • The hook is not only relevant - as this is regularly in the news - but also a very good motivation for your topic.
  • You combine material well in the SOTA section
  • The first part of your theoretical framework is included in a senseful manner in your methodology
  • Your idea of a feedback loop, including feedback from students and teachers to refine the system, makes sense when developing a prototype

Weaknesses:

  • When making claims, they should be either marked as such or proven (e.g. by referencing existing material). Especially in your motivation section, you lack some references (e.g. "In Germany, one of the key reasons behind this..."). Even though this is not the SOTA section, back up your claims with references
  • A good research question is narrow enough to be answered completely. Your RQ is very open, and the Sub-RQs only partially answer it, and broaden your scope even more. Focus on a clear and concrete research questions, with Sub-questions that address a specific part of your RQ.
  • The methodology presented should make clear how the RQs will be answered. Even though you present a high level of detail in your methodology, the methods don't seem to be linked to your RQs and are as such mostly irrelevant - for example the AI platform has not even been mentioned in your RQs. Either adjust your RQs to align with your actual research goal, or you tailor your methods towards the RQs. This is a really important point for a good expose!
  • A theoretical framework presented should be relevant to your research. The second framework you mention is neither referenced later nor relevant to your methodology in my opinion. Include only relevant frameworks, or base your research closer on such frameworks.
