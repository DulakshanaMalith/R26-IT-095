Summary:

In this expose, the author highlights the issue of limited capabilities/availability of educators to provide personalized materials for a student’s education. They highlight that, with the usage of LLMs, exercise sheets that are custom to a student’s strengths and weaknesses are possible. To prove such a claim, they propose to conduct a qualitative study on this approach’s effectiveness by surveying participants and employing prompting strategies

Strengths:

  • Very well written, it was easy to follow the author’s arguments and framing of the project
  • I appreciate the focus on human feedback in this project

Weaknesses:

  • I felt that the author did not make any proper mention of how they plan to recruit people for their study and how they intend to ensure responses are of high quality
