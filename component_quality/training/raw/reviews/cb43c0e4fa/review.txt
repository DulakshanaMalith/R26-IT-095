Weaknesses
(Schedule)
The schedule outlines key milestones but does not comprehensively address all of the research questions and is not complete. Without explicitly linking the tasks in the schedule to each sub-research question and making sure that there are no undefined timelines, the proposal risks a lack of focus and coherence in achieving its objectives. Add the Sub RQ 1.2 into the schedule and complete the months 4,5,6 as commented.
(Language)
The language in scientific work should be precise, formal, and objective to communicate ideas clearly and credibly, without trying the reader to convince. However, in this proposal there are some areas where the language could be further refined. For example avoid using “we”, “my” etc.. Instead, consider make it more neutral, objective. I recommend reviewing the text to eliminate subjective language. 
(Citations)
In scientific writing, one of the most important things is citing the claims and ideas to provide evidence, demonstrate engagement, give credit to the work of other researchers and make your own work more credible for readers. The proposal lacks of citations to support the claims made. For instance, there is no citations in your motivation. Without citations, the points you raise come across as opinions rather than evidence-based insights, which can weaken the academic value of your work. The proposal schould be supported with more scientific research.
