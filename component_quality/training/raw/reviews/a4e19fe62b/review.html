Summary

This proposal aims at analyzing sitting places for holographic AI. Although current methods can detect objects and its bounding box, they are incapable of analysing the exact parameters of a sitting place. This proposal aims to developing methods to determine the orientation and dimensions of a suitable sitting place. This proposal develops a method to leverage procedural generation in reinforcement learning to solve this problem.

Strengths

  • This proposal identifies an important question that the exact parameters of a sitting place haven’t been explored by the current studies. 
  • This proposal has a manageable timetable.

Weakness

  • The example given in the motivation as a hooker is hard to understand for people not familiar with the topic. To give a good hooker, the author should give some context and background of the topic first.  
  • The common thread is not clear enough, and the text is fragmented, making it hard to understand the real question that the author wants to solve. Rather than using a section title to highlight the problem was it dealt with so far, the author incorporates the problem into the existing work, incorporating intothe paragraph smoothly 
  • The drawback listed in the introduction part should be connected to research questions that need to be solved in this project.
  • The methodology part does not connect the research questions closely. How the research questions are solved using the proposed method is not stated.
  • The theoretical framework is lacking. The proposed method should be grounded in the theoretical framework.
