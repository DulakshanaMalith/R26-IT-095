Summary:

The exposé describes a study that uses Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to generate synthetic data on disease outbreaks with the aim of developing realistic and privacy-compliant models of health crises.

Strength:

  • The motivation should explain to the reader why the chosen topic is relevant and which weakness the thesis wants to address. The structure of the motivation is clear and easy to follow, which is important to pique the reader's interest. The examples help the reader understand the significance of your research.
  • Within the theoretical framework, the theories used in the work are presented and their relevance justified.The framework is well written, easy to understand and well linked to the overall topic and methods.
  • The methodology describes how the individual research questions are answered. Your methodology is very well structured. In particular, the limitations and their solutions are very good.

Weakness:

  • The hook serves to catch the reader's interest and get them to read the exposé. Although your exposé is well structured, it lacks a strong hook to grab the reader's attention. Think about what fact about the problem you want to address in your research could be interesting or shocking. The best way to do this is to search for facts and statistics.
  • It is important to effectively communicate the problem in the motivation section in order to demonstrate the relevance and significance of the research to the reader. Although the background of the problem is well explained in your work, the overall problem and the relevance of the research need to be explained further. To do this, reread your motivation and try to expand it with examples and further arguments.
  • It is very important to make it clear in an exposé what the aim of the work is in order to give the reader a precise understanding of the objectives. Your text still lacks formulated research questions. Think about the aim of your thesis and try to formulate it into a main research question. It is also helpful to split this into sub-questions to create a clear overview.
  • The SOTA should provide a clearer explanation of the underlying problem and its relevance. In addition, your text should show how your work differs from existing research. In your text, the state of the art is incomplete. It is best to take another look at the lecture on writing an exposé and use the example presented there as a guide.
  • The methodology describes how the individual research questions are answered. Although your methodology is very detailed, it could be structured more clearly. It contains a large amount of information that could overwhelm the reader and make it difficult for them to follow your methodology. Reread your methodology carefully, keeping in mind that it should also be understandable for readers who are not familiar with the subject area. Also try to remove some irrelevant information.
  • The time schedule is used to plan the thesis in advance to see whether it is possible in the short time available. It is therefore very important that all aspects of the thesis are included. The writing of the thesis is missing in your time schedule. Think about which chapters all belong in a thesis and when they should best be written.
