Despite its many strengths, the expose has a few areas that could be refined to enhance its overall quality.

Another area for potential improvement is the forward-looking perspective. While the current expose provides a comprehensive current analysis, it could be enhanced by including a brief outline of potential future research directions or strategies for scaling the framework beyond its initial scope.
