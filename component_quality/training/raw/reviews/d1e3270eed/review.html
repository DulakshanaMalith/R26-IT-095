Summary

Your work is about recognising depression with the help of LLMs. To this end, you want to investigate which additional indicators can be used for better detection and integrate them as additional modalities. Capabilities, limitations, ethical and practical considerations will also be analysed.

Strengths

Your exposé is really well written. It seems clear and understandable what the problem is and it is precisely presented what the subject of the investigation is and how the RQs are to be answered.

Weaknesses

  • The theoretical framework is intended to serve as a guide for your planned research. Although you have described a suitable theoretical framework, it remains unclear how this will guide your work. Think again about how the theoretical framework could be helpful in answering your research question or in applying your methods.
  • Thinking about the target group in advance also serves the purpose of realistically assessing whether it is possible to realise in your work. 200 people may be possible, but with such a large number you need to give it more thought. Consider whether this is not too much of a challenge in the context of a bachelor's thesis and whether fewer people might be sufficient. And is the target group suitable for recognising depression?
