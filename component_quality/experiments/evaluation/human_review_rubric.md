# Human Review Rubric for Exposía Reviewer Output

This rubric is designed for a blinded human evaluation of the reviews generated by the four AI system conditions. Evaluators should not know which system (LLM-only, RAG, ML, ML+RAG) produced the review they are scoring.

Evaluate each review on a scale from **1 (Poor)** to **5 (Excellent)** for the following criteria:

## 1. Correctness & Relevance (1-5)
- **5:** Identifies actual substantive weaknesses and strengths in the proposal. All identified issues are relevant and academically sound.
- **3:** Identifies some valid issues, but misses key points or includes minor/pedantic issues.
- **1:** Identifies issues that do not exist, completely misunderstands the proposal, or focuses entirely on irrelevant formatting/grammar.

## 2. Evidence Grounding (1-5)
- **5:** Every claim or weakness is perfectly grounded in an exact, relevant quote from the proposal.
- **3:** Most claims are grounded, but some quotes are slightly misaligned or missing context.
- **1:** Claims are ungrounded, quotes are fabricated (hallucinated), or quotes do not support the stated weakness.

## 3. Actionability of Feedback (1-5)
- **5:** Recommendations are highly specific, constructive, and provide clear guidance on how the author can improve the proposal.
- **3:** Recommendations are somewhat generic (e.g., "Add more detail") but still valid.
- **1:** Recommendations are useless, confusing, or contradictory.

## 4. Hallucination / Unsupported Claims (1-5) *[Note: 5 is Best/No Hallucination]*
- **5:** No fabricated information. The review strictly references only what is in the proposal.
- **3:** Minor assumptions made about the author's intent, or slightly stretching the proposal's claims.
- **1:** Blatant fabrication of facts, methods, or historical context not present in the proposal.

## 5. Overall Usefulness (1-5)
- **5:** The review is ready to be delivered to a student as a highly valuable piece of feedback.
- **3:** The review is okay, but requires human editing or filtering before delivery.
- **1:** The review is harmful, confusing, or useless to a student.

## Evaluation Protocol
1. Read the ground truth proposal.
2. Read the randomized system review.
3. Score each criterion 1-5.
4. Note any qualitative observations (e.g., "Reviewer fixated too heavily on X").
